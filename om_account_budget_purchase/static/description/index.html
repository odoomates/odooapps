Budget Control of Purchases for Odoo 20 Community

Warn the buyer — or stop the order — when a purchase order goes over budget, and show on every budget line what the confirmed purchase orders have already committed but not yet billed.

Committed

What is spent, and what is already promised

A Committed column next to the practical amount of every expense line of a confirmed budget: the confirmed purchase orders that this line counts and that are not billed yet. The budget shows what has been spent and what is about to be.

  • ✓  Only the part of an order line that is not billed yet is committed: once the vendor bill is posted, the amount moves to the practical column by itself.
  • ✓  An order line counts against a budget line through the expense account of its product and its analytic distribution, the same way the bills do.
  • ✓  Orders in a foreign currency are converted at the rate of the day the order was approved.
  • ✓  Refunds on a vendor bill give the quantity back to the committed amount.
Control

The buyer is told before the order is confirmed

A banner on the purchase order names the budget lines the order would push over their planned amount, while the order is still a request for quotation.

  • ✓  The warning follows the lines as they are typed: the products, the amounts and the analytic distribution.
  • ✓  With Block Documents Over Budget, confirming an order that goes over budget is refused and the lines at fault are listed.
  • ✓  An accounting manager can still confirm the order, or raise the budget.
  • ✓  Without blocking, the warning is shown and the buyer decides.
  • ✓  Installed by itself as soon as Purchase and the budgets are both there.
