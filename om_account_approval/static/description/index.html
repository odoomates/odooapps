Odoo 20 Accounting Approvals

Vendor bills, credit notes, journal entries and payments posted only once approved.

  • The approval flows of the Approvals app on the Post button hold for every way of posting: the Post action of the lists, the Confirm Entries wizard, the auto-post and the Register Payment wizard.
  • An approval is used up by the first post; reset to draft, the entry asks again.
  • The auto-post leaves the entries waiting for an approval aside and asks for it.
  • The entries Odoo posts itself are never stopped; journals can post without approval.
  • A Waiting for Approval filter, and accounting templates in the Approvals gallery.
