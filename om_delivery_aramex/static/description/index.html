Overview

From the quotation to the doorstep

Rates when you sell, the shipment when you deliver, the tracking until it arrives: all from the standard delivery methods of Odoo.

Live Rates
The Aramex rate on quotations and at the website checkout, in your currency.
Express and Domestic
Domestic when the parcel stays in the country, express otherwise, or always one of them; the product type you choose.
AWB and Label
Validating the delivery creates the Aramex shipment; the AWB is the tracking reference and the label is attached.
Cash on Delivery
COD when the order was paid with Cash on Delivery, always, or never, with the amount to collect.
Customs Value
Customs value and items with HS code and country of origin for international shipments.
Prepaid or Collect
Freight paid by you or collected from the consignee.
Packages
Number of pieces and weight from the packages of the delivery, dimensions from the package type.
Tracking
Aramex tracking link for you and the customer portal, and the latest status in the chatter.
Test and Production
The Aramex test services in the test environment, your live account in production.
Features

A closer look

Configuration

Your Aramex account

✓  Username, password, account number, PIN, entity and country, visible to administrators only.
✓  Product group and type, payment type, COD rule.
✓  Label layout and package type.
Sales

Rate on the quotation

✓  Add shipping, pick the delivery method: the rate comes from Aramex.
✓  The same rate at the website checkout.
✓  Margins and free shipping of Odoo apply.
Inventory

AWB and label on validation

✓  AWB as tracking reference, label PDF in the chatter.
✓  Status button for the latest tracking event.
✓  Aramex tracking page from the Tracking button.
Setup

Ready in a few minutes

1. Create the delivery method
Inventory, Configuration, Delivery Methods: choose Aramex as provider.
2. Enter your account
Your Aramex credentials in the Aramex Configuration tab, then Check Connection.
3. Set your options
Services, package type, cash on delivery, labels: each option has its help text.
4. Sell and ship
Add shipping on quotations, publish the method for the website, validate deliveries to ship.
