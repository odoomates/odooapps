Everything at a glance

Monthly Recurring Revenue
The MRR of the running subscriptions, for each month of the period.
New MRR
The MRR added by new subscriptions and upsells in the period; renewals do not count twice.
First Period Only
Only the first invoice, or one month of MRR, earns a commission.
Subscriptions Only
A plan for the subscriptions alone, next to the plans of the other sales.
