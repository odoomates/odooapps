=================================
Sales Commissions - Subscriptions
=================================

Commissions on subscriptions in Odoo 20 Community: links Sales Commissions with
Subscriptions and is installed automatically with both.

On every commission plan, in the Subscriptions tab:

* **Subscription Commission On**:

  * **Invoiced Amount**: the recurring lines of subscription invoices count like
    any other invoice line.
  * **Monthly Recurring Revenue**: the MRR of the subscriptions running at the
    end of each period, for each month of the period.
  * **New MRR**: the MRR added in the period by new subscriptions and upsells
    (renewals that take over a subscription do not count again).

* **Pay Subscriptions**: **Every Period**, or **First Period Only**: only the
  first invoice of a subscription (and its credit notes), or one month of MRR in
  the period the subscription starts.
* **Subscriptions Only**: the plan ignores the other sales.

The salesperson, sales team and referrer of the subscription earn the
commission. MRR commissions have no product: give the plan a rule on all
products, or use a plan with a target and achievement tiers.
