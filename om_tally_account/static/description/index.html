Tally Connector Accounting

Maps the groups and ledgers of Tally to the chart of accounts, links or creates the parties, journals and analytic accounts, and posts the opening balances and the vouchers after a trial balance check.

Part of Import from Tally (om_tally_connector).
Groups to Account Types
The 28 groups of Tally have defaults; your own groups inherit them.
Ledgers to Accounts
Link to the chart by name and type, or create accounts with generated codes.
Parties
Matched by GSTIN, name, phone, email; Link, Create or Skip with bulk buttons.
Bank and Cash
Bank and cash ledgers become journals.
Taxes
GST ledgers to CGST, SGST and IGST in India; VAT by rate in the Gulf.
New Odoo Company
From the details of Tally, with the fiscal year of its country.
Cost Centres
Cost categories and centres become analytic plans and accounts.
Trial Balance Check
Blocks posting until Tally and Odoo agree, or you accept the difference.
Batches in the Background
Progress, errors per voucher, resume, and no duplicates.

Map the chart

✓  Link, create or skip each ledger.
Parties
✓  Link, create or skip each debtor and creditor.
Check

Invoices, bills and payments

Post
✓  The opening entry first, then the vouchers in batches.
