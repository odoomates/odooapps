Bridge of the Point of Sale and the WhatsApp connector of Odoo Mates, installed by itself when both are.

What it does

Through the WhatsApp connector of Odoo Mates, with the provider of your choice.

In the POS
A WhatsApp button next to the email.
Receipt Picture
The receipt as the customer saw it; a PDF on Meta templates.
Invoice PDF
When the order is invoiced.
Customer Number
Filled with the number of the customer.
Click to Chat
Opens WhatsApp with the text filled in.
Per Point of Sale
Template and account of each shop.
On the Order
Each receipt kept in its chatter.
Safe
Opt-outs skipped; the cashier sees why.

See it at work

Screens from a test database with the demo data.

From the receipt screen
Type or keep the number of the customer and press the WhatsApp button.
✓  As for the email receipt, the order is synced first.
✓  The cashier is told when a receipt could not go, and why.
On the order
The receipt picture is sent and kept in the chatter of the order.
✓  With the invoice PDF when the order is invoiced.
✓  For the providers that send files.
Settings
Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings > Bills & Receipts.
✓  Turn on WhatsApp Receipts for each point of sale.
✓  Choose the template and the account to send from.

Works with the WhatsApp Connector

Needs the base; add one provider. Messages go by themselves with Meta and QR; WhatsApp Web sends them from the browser of the user, Click to Chat opens WhatsApp with the text filled in. With Meta, submit the templates for approval first.

WhatsApp Connector Base
Templates, composer and message log: needed by this app.
WhatsApp Connector Meta
The official Cloud API: sends by itself, approved templates.
WhatsApp Connector Web
Texts and PDFs from WhatsApp Web in your browser.
WhatsApp Connector QR
Your WhatsApp number linked by a QR code, sending by itself.
