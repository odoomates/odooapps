Sales Commissions

Pay your salespersons, sales teams and resellers a share of what they sell: commission plans, targets with achievement tiers, statements approved by the sales manager and paid with a vendor bill.

Salespersons, teams, resellers Revenue, margin, per unit Targets and tiers Statements and approval Vendor bills Clawback Reseller portal Community Edition Free, LGPL-3

Everything at a glance

Commissions are computed from the invoices or the sales orders you already have: nothing new to enter for your sales team.

Commission Plans
For salespersons, sales teams or resellers, monthly, quarterly or yearly, with a validity.
Flexible Rules
Percentage of revenue or margin, or a fixed amount per unit, per product, category or for all.
Targets and Tiers
Below 80%: 0%, from 80%: 3%, from 100%: 5% - with a target per person and a cap.
Team Splits
To the salesperson of the sale or equally between members, plus a team leader override.
Invoices or Orders
Count invoices when posted or when paid, or confirmed sales orders. Credit notes reduce the commission.
Statements
One statement per person and period, approved by the sales manager.
Vendor Bills
The accounting bills the commission to the salesperson or reseller; paid bill, paid statement.
Clawback
A cancelled or refunded invoice takes back a commission already paid, in the next statement.
A closer look

From the plan to the payment

Screenshots from an Odoo 20 Community database with the demo data of the app.

Commission plans with rules
A percentage of the revenue on all products and of the margin on services, for the salespersons of the plan. Invoices count when posted or when paid.
Targets and achievement tiers
A target per period, one for everybody or one per salesperson, and the rate of each tier of achievement. A cap limits the commission of one person.
Achievement dashboard
Revenue, target, share of the target reached, tier rate and commission of every salesperson, team and reseller, period by period.
Commission lines
One line per invoice line or order line, with the plan, the rate, the commission and a link to the invoice or the sales order.
Statements: draft, approved, paid
One statement per salesperson or reseller with every commission not settled yet. The sales manager approves it; the vendor bill pays it.
Paid with a vendor bill
The commission product, journal and expense account come from the settings. The statement is paid when the bill is paid.
Clawback
An invoice refunded or cancelled after the payout takes the commission back at its original rate, in the next statement.
Sales team plans
The commission of the team goes to the salesperson of the sale or is split equally between the members; the team leader earns an override.
Resellers and referrers
A referrer on the sales order, copied to its invoices and credit notes. Reseller grades such as Gold and Silver get their own plan.
Portal for resellers
Resellers with portal access follow their approved and paid statements and download them as PDF.
Commission analysis
Commissions per salesperson, team, plan and period, paid and pending, in pivot, graph and list.
Settings
Product, journal and expense account of the commission vendor bills.
Access rights
Salespersons see their own commissions, achievements and statements. Sales managers see everything, manage the plans and approve the statements. Invoicing users create the vendor bills.
Subscriptions and payroll
Install Subscriptions too and the add-on Sales Commissions - Subscriptions pays commissions on MRR or new MRR, every period or the first period only. Statements with the Other payout can be paid with another tool, such as payslips.

In four steps

1
Set the vendor bill
Sales > Settings > Commissions: the commission product and journal.
2
Create a plan
Commissions > Configuration > Plans: who, how, how much.
3
Sell
Commissions are computed every day from the invoices or the orders.
4
Approve and pay
Create the statements, approve them, bill and pay them.
