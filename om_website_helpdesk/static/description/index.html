Overview

From the website to the helpdesk

Help Page
A /helpdesk page with the teams that take requests, and a Help entry in the website menu.
Ticket Form
Name, email, phone, subject, description and files. Signed-in customers get their details filled in.
Customer Found or Created
The ticket is linked to the contact with the same email, or to a new contact.
Confirmation
The ticket number and a link to follow the ticket in the customer portal.
Per Team and Website
Turn the form on per team, on one website or on all the websites of the company.
Spam Protection
Built on the website form of Odoo: the reCAPTCHA and Turnstile modules protect it when installed.
