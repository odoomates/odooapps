====================================================
AI Expense Receipts - Read Receipts with Your Own AI
====================================================

Reads expense receipts with your own AI provider or a local model of the AI
engine.

* **Read Receipt with AI** on a draft expense with a receipt attached (a photo
  or a PDF): the merchant and what was bought (description), the date, the
  total and its currency, and the expense category (chosen among the expense
  products) are filled. The vendor is set when a contact has the merchant's
  name.
* **Read Receipts with AI** in the Actions menu of the list, for several
  expenses.
* *Settings > Expenses > Read Receipts with AI*: also read the expenses
  created by uploading receipts.
* What to check is shown on the expense: no category, future date, taxes that
  differ from the taxes computed, low confidence, flags of the AI. The
  expense stays a draft for the employee to submit.
* Usage is logged under the feature ``extract``.
