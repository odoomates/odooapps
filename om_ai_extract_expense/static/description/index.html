Your AI, your key
Built on the free AI Engine (om_ai): the requests go from your Odoo server straight to the provider you choose, with your key, or to a model server on your own machine. Nothing goes through Odoo Mates. Every call is in the AI usage log, within your daily limits.

Read Receipt with AI

On a draft expense with its receipt. What to check is shown on the expense: no category, a future date, taxes that differ, a low confidence.

Uploads too

Read the expenses created by uploading receipts, or several expenses at once from the list.
Good to know
  • Needs Expenses and AI Document Reading (installed together automatically).
  • Usage is logged under the feature "extract". Expenses are never submitted automatically.
