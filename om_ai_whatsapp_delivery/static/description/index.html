In the chat

The customer knows when it ships

No need to ask "where is my order?": the message comes when the delivery is validated.

Tracking in WhatsApp

The carrier and its tracking link, or the tracking reference when the carrier has none. Each delivery sends one message, a backorder its own when it leaves. The carrier connectors that fill the tracking reference work as they are.

On or off, with your words

Shipping Messages is on by default on each WhatsApp account with the AI assistant. The text is yours, translatable, with {order}, {delivery}, {carrier} and {tracking}.
Opt-out respected
A number that sent STOP gets nothing; the delivery says why in its chatter.
Person in charge
When a person took over, the notice still goes and a note says so in the conversation.
Order status
The assistant shows the carrier and the tracking link of each delivery when asked.
Never blocks
A message that cannot be sent never stops the validation of the delivery.
