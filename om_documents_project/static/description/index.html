Overview

Every project keeps its files

Folder per Project
Created in a Projects folder, renamed with the project.
Task Files
Files attached to the project and its tasks land in its folder.
Share with the Customer
One click shares the folder; the customer finds it in the portal.
Features

A closer look

Project

Documents and Share Documents

✓  A Documents button on the projects and the tasks.
✓  A setting per project to keep no documents.
Folder

The files of the project and its tasks

✓  Each file linked to its task, the customer as its contact.
✓  Upload more files in the folder of the project.
Share

Shared with the customer

✓  The folder and its subfolders, valid until a date, with download or upload.
✓  Sent by email; shown in the portal of the customer.
Suite

Documents and its bridges

Documents FREE
Folders with access rights, preview, versions, share and upload links, requests, actions.
Documents - Accounting FREE
Bills, invoices and credit notes from documents; invoice files in a folder.
Documents - Sign FREE
Signature requests from PDF documents; the signed PDF as the new version.
Documents - Project FREE
A folder per project with the files of its tasks, shared with the customer.
Documents - Product FREE
A folder per product with its sheets, manuals and pictures.
Documents - Expiry FREE
Document types, expiry dates, reminders, renewals and a Documents widget on records.
