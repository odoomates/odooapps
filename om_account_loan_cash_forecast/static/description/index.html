Loans and Leases in the Cash Forecast

Links Loans and Leases with Cash Forecast: the instalments still to pay and the lease payments still to bill are expected in the cash forecast on their due dates.

Installs by itself Loan instalments Lease payments
Loans and Leases Cash Forecast
What it adds

Two more rows in the payments

✓ Loan instalments: the unpaid instalments of the running loans, on their due dates, for the instalment less the tax withheld (what leaves the bank).
✓ Lease payments not yet billed: the payments of the running leases, on their due dates, with the taxes of the bills. Once billed, a payment is a vendor bill of the forecast.
✓ In the company currency, at the rate of the starting date of the forecast, as the other items.
✓ The instalments already due follow the Overdue option of the forecast.
✓ Draft and closed loans, and paid instalments (in the bank already), are left out.
✓ Detail by Partner shows each lender and lessor.

The cash forecast by month: Loan instalments and Lease payments not yet billed among the payments.

Detail by Partner: each lender and lessor on its own line.

Install

Nothing to do, most of the time

✓ It needs Loans and Leases and Odoo 20 Cash Forecast.
✓ Take the latest Odoo 20 Cash Forecast: the bridge needs what this release adds to it (the rows of the forecast are extendable). Download it again, replace its folder and upgrade it first.
✓ It installs by itself when both apps are installed. If they already are, install it from the Apps menu (developer mode, Update Apps List, search om_account_loan_cash_forecast).
✓ The menus are under Invoicing › Accounting, shown to the users with Show Full Accounting Features.
