Tally Connector Inventory

Brings the inventory masters of Tally into Odoo: units, product categories, products, godowns as locations, batches as lots, and the opening stock at the value of Tally.

FreeProductsGodownsBatchesOpening stockLGPL-3
Part of Import from Tally (om_tally_connector).
Units
Nos, Kg, Ltr and the others matched with the units of Odoo.
Categories
Stock groups or stock categories as product categories; the other as tags.
Products
Matched by part number, name or HSN: Link, Create or Skip.
Godowns
Godowns become locations of a warehouse.
Batches
Batch-wise items are tracked by lots.
Opening Stock
Per godown and batch, at the rate of Tally.
GST Rate
Products get the GST taxes of their rate.
Invoice Lines
Invoices made from vouchers get their products and units.
