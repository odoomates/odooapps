Report Designer for Odoo 20

Draw a report on its page with the mouse, without code. The same layout prints as a PDF from the Print menu and shows on screen from a menu.

What you draw is what prints

The page is shown at its real size, in A4, A5, Letter or Legal, portrait or landscape, with its margins.

Bands

A page header and footer on every page, a report header and footer once, and record bands repeated for each record.

Drag your fields

Drop the fields of the model on a band with their label, and the fields of related records, such as the phone of the customer.

More than fields

Add texts, lines, boxes, the company logo, the page number and the print date.

Tables of lines

Drop a list of lines, such as the Order Lines of a sales order, on a record band.

Columns

Drag the fields of the lines into the table, move and resize a column, and double click its title to rename it.

Totals

A column can show its total under the lines.

Filter and sort

Choose which lines appear and in what order.

Arrange and style

Move elements with the mouse or the arrow keys. They snap to a grid in millimetres and to the edges of other elements.

Align together

Align, size and space several elements at once.

Style

Fonts, sizes, bold, italic, underline, colors, borders, backgrounds and alignment, with formats for numbers, amounts, dates and images.

Work safely

Undo and redo, copy, paste and duplicate, and a preview with a real record before saving.

Good to know

What to expect before you install.

  • Publishing adds the report to the Print menu of its records and, when you choose a menu, shows it on screen for the records of a filter, with a button to download the PDF.
  • The report reads the records as the user who prints it, so their access rules and company apply.
  • Only members of the group Report Designer / Designer design and publish reports. You can limit each report to some groups.
