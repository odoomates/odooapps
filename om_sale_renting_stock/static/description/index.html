Rental - Stock

Rental with Inventory: pickups move the products to a rental location, returns bring them back, with serial numbers.

Rental location per warehouseSerial numbersReal stock availabilityCommunity EditionFree, LGPL-3
Installed automatically with Rental and Inventory.

What it adds

Rental Location
One per warehouse, outside its stock.
Stock Moves
Pickup and return move the stock, linked to the order line.
Serial Numbers
Reserve them, choose them at pickup, get the same back.
Availability
Units in the warehouse and out on rental, minus the bookings.
Serials at pickup
Free serials of the warehouse are proposed, reserved ones first.
✓  Serials reserved by other rentals of the period are left out.
✓  Lots and untracked products work too.
Out on rental
Picked up serials on the order and a smart button for the moves.
✓  Return moves the serials back to the warehouse.
✓  No delivery orders for rental lines.
