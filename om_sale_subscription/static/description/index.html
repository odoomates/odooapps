Subscriptions

Sell recurring services from your sales orders: plans, invoices every period, automatic payments, upsell and renewal, a customer portal and MRR reports.

Recurring plans Automatic invoices Saved payment methods Upsell and renewal Customer portal MRR, ARR, churn Community Edition Free, LGPL-3

Everything at a glance

A subscription is a sales order with a recurring plan: nothing new to learn for your sales team.

Recurring Plans
Every N days, weeks, months or years, in advance or at the end of the period.
Prices per Plan
Monthly and yearly prices on the product, per variant and pricelist.
Automatic Invoices
One invoice per period with its dates, posted and sent, or left in draft.
Automatic Payments
The saved payment method is charged; failed payments are tried again.
Upsell
Add quantities in the middle of a period, prorated to the next invoice.
Renewal
Renew automatically, or send a renewal quotation before the end of the term.
Customer Portal
Customers see their subscriptions, pay, change the payment method, pause or close.
MRR Reports
MRR over time, new, expansion, contraction and churn, retention by month.
A closer look

From the quotation to the recurring revenue

Screenshots from an Odoo 20 Community database with the demo data of the app.

All your subscriptions
Plan, next invoice, recurring total, MRR, health and status of each subscription, as a list, kanban, pivot or graph.
A sales order that repeats
Pick a recurring plan on the quotation and confirm it: the start date, the next invoice and the MRR are set, and the buttons to invoice, upsell, renew, pause and close appear.
Plans that do the follow-up
Billing period, invoices posted and sent or left in draft for a check, term and what happens at its end, payment retries, closing after unpaid invoices, and what customers may do from the portal.
Recurring prices on the product
Tick Subscription on a product and give it a price for each plan, per pricelist if you need it.
One invoice per period
The scheduled action invoices each due subscription with the dates of the period, posts and sends the invoice and charges the saved payment method.
Upsell in the middle of a period
The upsell quotation lists the current lines: added quantities are invoiced for the rest of the period and added to the subscription for the next ones.
Close with a reason
Every closing keeps its reason and note, for the churn reports; the customer gets an email.
Customers serve themselves
My Subscriptions in the portal: plan, lines, invoices to pay, the payment method charged, pause, resume and close when the plan allows it.
MRR over time
Every change of recurring revenue is logged with its date: the cumulated graph is your MRR month after month.
Where the MRR comes from
New, expansion, contraction, churn and renewals, month by month.
Retention by start month
Subscriptions grouped by the month they started, with the share still running and the share closed.

In three steps

1
Create a plan
Subscriptions > Configuration > Recurring Plans: monthly and yearly plans come with the app.
2
Price your products
Tick Subscription on a product and add its price for each plan.
3
Sell
Make a quotation with a plan and confirm it, or let the customer accept and pay it online.
