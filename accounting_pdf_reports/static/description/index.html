What it shows

✓Balance Sheet and Profit and Loss with last year and the variance.
✓Optional Cash Flow Statement, notes and approval page.

Show Variancethe movement in amount and per cent

Download and install

Up and running in five minutes

The app is free, for Odoo 20.0 Community. It needs only the Invoicing app (account) and no extra Python library.

1
From this page
  1. Click Download at the top of this page.
  2. Unzip it into a folder of your Odoo addons path.
  3. Restart the Odoo server.
2
From GitHub
  1. Clone the 20.0 branch with the command below, or download it as a zip.
  2. Add the folder to the addons path (or copy only accounting_pdf_reports).
  3. Restart the server.
3
Install in Odoo
  1. Turn on the developer mode.
  2. Apps › Update Apps List.
  3. Remove the Apps filter, search accounting_pdf_reports, click Activate.
The GitHub way, in a terminal:
git clone -b 20.0 --depth 1 https://github.com/odoomates/odooapps.git

Upgrading? Replace the folder, restart the server, then click Upgrade on the app. Want everything? Download Odoo 20 Accounting Community instead: it includes this module. Odoo.sh? Add the repository as a submodule of your branch.

How to use

Your reports, step by step

Where each report is and what it gives you. The administrator sees every report; a user with Billing rights sees the Partner Reports. The Accounting menu of Invoicing (journal entries, ledger items) shows to the users with Show Full Accounting Features: tick it on your user in developer mode.

1
Open the reports
Once the app is installed, every report is in Invoicing › Reporting. Each one opens a short form: set the dates and the filters, then click Print for the PDF.
2
Balance Sheet
Statement Reports › Balance Sheet: the assets, the liabilities and the equity at the end of the period. Tick Enable Comparison for a second column.
3
Profit and Loss
Statement Reports › Profit and Loss: the income, the expenses and the net profit. Tick Display Debit/Credit Columns for the detail.
4
Cash Flow Statement
Statement Reports › Cash Flow Statement: from the opening to the closing cash, through the operating, investing and financing activities.
5
General Ledger and Trial Balance
Audit Reports › General Ledger with Include Initial Balances for the movements of each account, then Audit Reports › Trial Balance for one line per account.
6
Partner Ledger and ageing
Partner Reports › Partner Ledger for the entries of each customer and vendor, Partner Reports › Aged Partner Balance for what is late. From a contact, Print › Balance Statement gives a statement to send.
7
Tax Report
Taxes & Fiscal › Tax Report: pick a period, print By Tax or By Tax Grid. Close Tax Period prepares the draft entry that clears the tax accounts.
8
Annual Report
Statement Reports › Annual Report: filled in for the current financial year; tick the sections you want and print the year-end pack.
9
Journals
Audit Reports › Journals Audit for the entries of each journal; on invoices, bills and entries, Print › Journals Entries prints the ones you selected.
Something missing, or an idea to make it better? Let us know on GitHub or at odoomates@gmail.com: we are happy to make it.
