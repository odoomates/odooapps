Annual Report

The whole year, in one document

One PDF for the financial year: a cover page, a table of contents, the key figures, then the Balance Sheet and the Profit and Loss, each beside the same period a year earlier.

  • ◆Opens on the current financial year, already filled in.
  • ◆Last year in the next column, with the movement in amount and per cent.
  • ◆A contents page that names the page each statement starts on.
  • ◆Your company logo on the cover, and the key figures on a page of their own.
  • ◆Add the Cash Flow Statement, your own notes and a page to sign.

The cover, and the key figures with last year beside them.

Statements

The three statements, on any period

Balance Sheet, Profit and Loss and Cash Flow Statement, over the dates you choose, with a comparison column and the level of detail you want.

  • ◆Posted entries only, or the drafts as well.
  • ◆Compare with the previous period or the same period last year.
  • ◆Show the accounts, the account types, or only the totals.
  • ◆Built from the account types, so they work with any chart of accounts.
  • ◆Branches: the Balance Sheet of the parent company includes them.

The Balance Sheet and the Profit and Loss.

Ledgers

Every entry, account by account and partner by partner

The General Ledger prints the movements of each account with its opening and closing balance; the Partner Ledger does the same customer by customer and vendor by vendor.

  • ◆Filter on journals, accounts or partners.
  • ◆Initial balance carried in when a start date is set.
  • ◆Receivable, payable, or both; reconciled entries in or out; a currency column.
  • ◆The Trial Balance sums it all up, one line per account.

The General Ledger and the Partner Ledger.

Ageing

How old the open balances are

The Aged Partner Balance spreads what is open across ageing buckets, so you can see at a glance who is late and by how much.

  • ◆Any interval length, in days.
  • ◆Aged Receivable and Aged Payable as reports of their own.
  • ◆At any date, not only today.

The Aged Partner Balance and the Trial Balance.

Tax and audit

What the tax office and the auditor open first

The Tax Report gives the tax collected and paid for the period, by tax or as the tax return of your country; the Journals Audit prints the entries of each journal in order with their tax detail.

  • ◆Period shortcuts: this or last month, quarter or financial year.
  • ◆Cash basis taxes counted in the period of the payment.
  • ◆Withholding taxes in a section of their own, their base counted once.
  • ◆By Tax Grid: the return of your localization, grid by grid, ready to copy into the tax portal.
  • ◆Close Tax Period: a draft entry that clears the tax accounts against the tax payable or receivable.

The Tax Report and the Journals Audit.

Download and install

Up and running in five minutes

The app is free. Pick the way that suits your server; all of them give the same module, accounting_pdf_reports, for Odoo 20.0 Community. It needs only the Invoicing app (account) and no extra Python library.

1
From this page
  1. Click Download at the top of this page and save the zip.
  2. Unzip it into a folder of your Odoo addons path.
  3. Restart the Odoo server.
2
From GitHub
  1. Clone the 20.0 branch:git clone -b 20.0 --depth 1 https://github.com/odoomates/odooapps.git
  2. Or download it as a zip.
  3. Add the folder to the addons path (or copy only accounting_pdf_reports) and restart.
3
Install in Odoo
  1. Turn on the developer mode (Settings, at the bottom).
  2. Apps › Update Apps List.
  3. Remove the Apps filter, search accounting_pdf_reports and click Activate.

Upgrading? Replace the folder with the new one, restart the server, then click Upgrade on the app in Apps. Want everything? Download Odoo 20 Accounting Community instead: it includes this module. Odoo.sh? Add the repository as a submodule of your branch, as for any third-party module.

Test it

Try every report in fifteen minutes

A walk through the app on a test database, with what to check on each report. The administrator sees every report; a user with Billing rights only sees the Partner Reports. The Accounting menu of Invoicing (journal entries, ledger items) shows to the users with Show Full Accounting Features: tick it on your user in developer mode, Settings › Users.

1
Start from a test database
Create a new database with demonstration data ticked, and install Invoicing: its demo invoices, bills and payments fill every report. Install the app as above, logged in as the administrator.
2
Balance Sheet
Open Invoicing › Reporting › Statement Reports › Balance Sheet, keep the dates and click Print. Check: the total assets equal the liabilities plus the equity. Tick Enable Comparison to add a second column.
3
Profit and Loss
Open Statement Reports › Profit and Loss and print the current year. Tick Display Debit/Credit Columns for the detail. Check: the net profit is the one that lands in the equity of the Balance Sheet.
4
Cash Flow Statement
Open Statement Reports › Cash Flow Statement. Check: the opening cash plus the net change of the three activities gives the closing cash.
5
General Ledger and Trial Balance
Print Audit Reports › General Ledger with Include Initial Balances, then Audit Reports › Trial Balance. Check: the debits and credits of the Trial Balance are equal.
6
Partner Ledger and ageing
Print Partner Reports › Partner Ledger and Partner Reports › Aged Partner Balance at today. Check: a customer with an unpaid demo invoice shows the same open amount in both. Also try Print › Balance Statement on the contact.
7
Tax Report
Open Taxes & Fiscal › Tax Report, pick a period and print By Tax, then By Tax Grid if your localization has a tax return. Close Tax Period prepares a draft closing entry: look at it, and discard it on a test database.
8
Annual Report
Open Statement Reports › Annual Report: it is filled in for the current financial year. Print it. Check: the contents page names the page each statement starts on, and the key figures agree with the Balance Sheet and the Profit and Loss you printed.
9
Journals
Print Audit Reports › Journals Audit, then select a few invoices in Invoicing › Customers › Invoices and use Print › Journals Entries.
Something does not add up? Open an issue on GitHub with the report, its options and what you expected, or write to odoomates@gmail.com.
Configuration

Ready on install, yours to adjust

  • ◆A default Balance Sheet and Profit and Loss structure is installed: print right away.
  • ◆Change what a statement contains in Invoicing › Configuration › Accounting › Financial Reports: parent and child lines, a sign, a display style and a level of detail.
  • ◆Put an account in another section of the Cash Flow Statement with the Cash Flow Activity field of the account.
  • ◆Every report filters by company, journals, dates and target moves: posted entries or all entries.
Questions

Before you install

Is it really free?
Yes. The app is LGPL-3 and costs nothing. The on-screen and Excel versions are separate, paid extensions, and you do not need them to print these reports.
Do I need the full Accounting app?
No. This module installs alone, on top of Invoicing. It is also part of Odoo 20 Accounting Community: install that later and it adds the rest around the reports you already have.
Which Odoo?
Odoo 20.0 Community Edition, with the Invoicing app. Versions for earlier Odoo releases are on their own pages of the store.
Does it work with my country?
Yes. The statements are built from the account types, so any chart of accounts works, and the Tax Report prints the tax return of your localization grid by grid when it ships one.
In my language?
The app is translated into 32 languages, from Arabic and Chinese to Spanish and Vietnamese.
Several companies?
Each report prints for the company you choose; the Balance Sheet of a parent company includes its branches.
