==========================
Inventory Reports - Expiry
==========================

Adds a **Lot Expiry** report to the Inventory Reports: the lots and serial
numbers in stock by expiration date, grouped as expired, alert date reached,
expiring soon and later, with the days left, the best before and removal dates.

* Choose how many days "expiring soon" covers.
* Key figures: lots in stock, expired lots and units, lots expiring soon.
* Filters, grouping, saved views and export to PDF and Excel as in the other
  inventory reports.

Installs on its own with Inventory Reports when product_expiry is installed.
