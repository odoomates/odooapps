Lot Expiry

What expires, where, and when

Grouped by expiry window, product and lot by default, or by category, warehouse and location. The days left are shown in red once expired and in orange when the expiry is near.

✓Choose what “expiring soon” means, in days.
✓Key figures: lots in stock, expired lots and units, lots expiring soon; click one to see only those lots.
✓Best before and removal dates as optional columns.

Install

Installs on its own with Inventory Reports when expiration dates (product_expiry) are installed. The report is in Inventory › Reporting › Inventory Reports › Lot Expiry.
