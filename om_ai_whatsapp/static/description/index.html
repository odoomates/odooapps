WhatsApp AI Sales Assistant

Your customers chat with your WhatsApp number and an AI assistant answers from live Odoo data: products, prices, stock, quotations with an online payment link, order status, invoices and balance. A person takes over in one click.

The AI never decides who the customer is
The code works out who writes from the WhatsApp number (and, if you want, from a 6-digit code mailed to the customer before invoices and balances). Tools always take the customer from the conversation, never from what the AI sends: nobody gets the orders or invoices of someone else, whatever they ask.
In the chat

From a question to a paid order

The assistant answers in the language of the customer, builds the quotation line by line and sends the standard Odoo payment link. The order confirms when it is paid.

Order on WhatsApp

Products found by name or code with the price of the customer's price list and the stock ("only 2 available, add 2?"). New customers are not asked to register: the assistant offers their WhatsApp profile name ("Shall I put the order under Emma Johnson?"), asks the delivery address, offers your delivery methods with their price, then shows a summary written by Odoo, not by the AI, with the taxes and the total. The customer replies YES.

Quotation PDF and payment link

After the YES the customer gets the quotation PDF and the online payment link of Odoo. Paying confirms the order the usual way and the customer gets a WhatsApp confirmation. One reminder goes if the link is still unpaid after the hours you set. No cash on delivery.

A real quotation in Sales

Draft quotations made by the assistant carry the source WhatsApp, the salesperson of the customer (or the default of the account) who is mentioned in the chatter, and say the assistant made them.
Trust levels

Each customer sees only what they may see

Levels are computed by Odoo from the conversation; every tool declares the level it needs.

Unknown number
A number without a contact, or on several contacts: products, public prices, in stock or not, your FAQ text.
Known number
The number is on one contact, or the contact was made in the chat: own quotations, payment links, order and delivery status.
Private data
Invoices, open balance, invoice payment links, addresses: for a known number, or only after the email code if you switch it on.
Optional email code
A 6-digit code mailed to the email on the contact, valid 30 days per number; 3 wrong codes lock it for one hour. Staff can tick or untick it on the contact.

Invoices, with an email code if you want

Asked for an invoice, the assistant sends the PDF with a payment link. With the setting Email Code for Private Data, it first mails a code to the email already on the contact: the right code verifies the number, wrong codes are counted and lock it. Each verification is logged in the chatter of the contact.

WhatsApp Verified on the contact

A box on the contact shows whether the number is verified; staff can set or remove it by hand.
Your rules

Settings per WhatsApp number

Switch the assistant on in a WhatsApp account and decide how it works.

Persona and knowledge
How the assistant speaks, and the text it answers general questions from: opening hours, delivery areas, returns.
Tools and levels
Enable only the tools you want. Actions that send a quotation wait for the YES of the customer.
Working hours
The assistant answers always, or only outside the working hours of your team.
Daily limits
Replies per day per number and per customer, on top of the AI token and cost limits of the AI engine.
Delivery methods
The delivery methods offered, priced by Odoo for the address of the customer.
Payment reminder
One WhatsApp reminder when a payment link is still unpaid after the hours you set.
Private data
An optional email code before invoices and balances, and own documents or those of the whole company.
Check Setup
One button tells what still keeps the assistant from answering.

One tab on the WhatsApp account

Salesperson, delivery methods, reminder, working hours, hand-over words, limits, private data, persona, knowledge and the enabled tools with their level.
People stay in charge

Hand-over in one click

A customer asking for a person, a doubt of the AI, a daily limit or an error: the conversation goes to the salesperson, who is added to it and notified, and the assistant stops.

Take over and give back

In Discuss, the hand button takes over and the assistant stops; give it back when you are done. A user who writes to the customer takes over too. STOP opts the number out and START opts it in again.

Reports

Conversations handled by the assistant, hand-overs, quotations and orders made, revenue of the confirmed orders and the AI cost of each conversation, from the usage log of the AI engine.
Built on

Works with every WhatsApp provider

The assistant hooks into the messages every provider of the WhatsApp Connector Base receives, and runs on the AI engine with your own provider and key.

Meta Cloud API
The official WhatsApp Business Platform, through the WhatsApp (Meta) module.
QR device
Your own number linked with a QR code, through the WhatsApp QR module.
WhatsApp Web
Answers go through the browser of the user who has WhatsApp Web open with the extension.
AI engine
OpenAI compatible APIs, Claude, Gemini, Azure OpenAI or a local model, with a usage log and limits.
Good to know
  • Needs the AI Engine with a chat model, the WhatsApp Connector Base with a provider that sends by itself (or WhatsApp Web open), Sales, and an online payment provider.
  • The assistant works as a WhatsApp Assistant user of the company with no password. It never confirms orders nor posts invoices.
  • Photos sent by customers stay in the conversation; they are not recognised. Product choices are numbered lists that every provider can send.
  • AI costs are billed by your AI provider; the estimates come from the prices you enter in the AI engine.
