Everything at a glance

Drag the steps onto the chart, link them, set each one. A workflow runs on the records of one model: quotations, purchases, invoices, leads, contacts, tasks, or any model with a chatter.

Start and flow
Start
When a record is created or changes, around one of its dates, when a form button is clicked, or by hand.
Condition
Yes or No, with Odoo's own condition editor: an amount, a status, a salesperson, a field of the lines.
Wait and Wait Until
Minutes, days or months, until a date, or until a condition holds, with a time limit and its own exit.
Stop condition
Checked at every step and while waiting: the invoice is paid, and the reminders stop on their own.
Approvals
Approval
A user, or the user of a field, approves or rejects; each answer has its exit, and so has no answer in time.
Approval on a button
Asked when Confirm, Post or Validate is clicked, not while the record is still being drafted.
Block until approved
The record shows the question at its top; its status, chosen fields or any change wait for the answer.
Answer for someone away
Administrators can answer for an approver who is away, and the approver is told.
Actions
Update the record
Set fields of the record, or create a linked record.
Send an email
Any email template, with placeholders such as {{ object.name }}.
Schedule an activity
A call, a to-do or any activity type, for a user or the user of a field.
Post a message
A note or a message in the chatter of the record.
Safe to change
Checked as you draw
Each step lists what it misses; a workflow with errors, or a loop without a wait, cannot be published.
Versions
Publishing freezes the chart; records on their way go on with the version they started on.
Runs and retry
See where each record is, cancel a run, or retry the step that failed. A failed step is rolled back alone.
Your own templates
Save a workflow as a template, export it as a file and import it into another database.

Approvals

The record waits, and says why

A record waiting for an approval shows it at its top, with Approve and Reject for the approver, and the chatter notes the question and the answer. While it waits, its status cannot change: the quotation cannot be confirmed or sent. Choose the fields to block, or any change. A step can keep the answer to the approver alone.

1
The salesperson drafts
Save the quotation as often as needed; nothing is asked.
2
Confirm asks
Clicking Confirm sends it for approval, and Confirm waits.
3
Approved, it goes on
The next click confirms. A bigger discount after the approval asks again.

Only a click is checked: Odoo itself, such as an online payment confirming an order, is never stopped.

Confirm is refused until the discount is approved. Lower the discount and the approval is withdrawn.

Every approver also finds their questions in Approvals, and as an activity.

Runs

Follow every record on the chart

Every record going through a workflow is a run: the step it is on, when it goes on, and each step it went through. Show it on the chart, open its record, cancel it, or retry the step that failed. A step that fails is rolled back alone: it never undoes the change that started the run.

Your templates

Keep what works, take it to another database

Save as Template keeps a workflow for your company, or for all of them, and it appears among the templates of New. Export a template as a file and import it into another database: users, activity types, email templates and the records the steps set are found again by their external id or their name, and what is missing is listed for you to choose again.

Get going

How to use it

1Automation Studio → Workflows → New: pick a template and the model it runs on, or start from blank.
2Adjust the chart in the designer: add steps, link them, set each one.
3Publish. From then on, every record that matches the Start goes through it.
4Follow the records in Runs, and answer the approvals on the record or in Approvals.
Good to know

How it works

✓  Built on Odoo's own Automation Rules: a published Start is an automation rule, so it starts exactly as Odoo's automations do. A Start on a button is checked when the button is clicked.
✓  Its own user groups: User sees the app and their approvals, Supervisor follows all runs and approvals, Administrator designs and publishes the workflows. The administrator gets it at install. Without a group the app is hidden, and approvers answer from the record itself.
✓  Waiting records go on by a scheduled action, every five minutes and at the exact time of a wait.
✓  With demo data, the ready templates of Sales, CRM, Purchase, Invoicing and Contacts are added as draft workflows, for the apps installed.
✓  It depends on nothing but the standard Automation Rules module: it works with any set of apps.
Get started

How to install it

1Copy the module to a folder of your own modules, e.g. /opt/odoo/custom-addons, and add that folder to addons_path in odoo.conf.
2Restart Odoo.
3Activate the developer mode, then Apps → Update Apps List.
4Clear the Apps filter, search Automation Studio and install it. Automation Rules comes with it.
Do not use Apps → Import Module. It is meant for data modules without Python, and leaves this module half loaded.
Still failing? Write to odoomates@gmail.com with your Odoo version and the error.

Questions we get

How is it different from Automation Rules?
A rule does one thing when something happens. A workflow goes on over days: it waits, asks, branches on the answer and remembers where each record is, and you see it all on one chart.
What if I change a workflow while records are in it?
Nothing breaks: they go on with the version they started on, and the new records follow the new version.
What if a step fails, for instance a missing email template?
Only that step is rolled back and the run stops as Failed, with the error. Fix the cause and retry it from the run.
Why ask on the button rather than on save?
A quotation changes many times before it is final. Asking on Confirm sends the manager only the figures the salesperson stands behind; asking on save remains possible for records with no button to confirm them, such as a customer's credit limit.
Can the approver be someone who cannot see the record?
With the User group of Automation Studio they answer from Approvals, and the record warns about it. Better: choose an approver who sees the records, such as a sales manager.
Is it really free?
Yes, all of it, under LGPL-3. There is no paid edition. Reviews, bug reports and ideas are welcome.
