Overview

Everything a support team needs

Tickets come in by email, from the website or from your agents; they are assigned, followed through stages and answered from the chatter.

Teams and Email Alias
Each team has its members, its stages and an email address: an email creates a ticket, the replies go to the same ticket.
Automatic Assignment
Manual, to the member with the fewest open tickets, or round robin.
SLA Policies
By priority, type, tags and customer: a stage to reach within working hours of the team calendar, or calendar hours.
Stage Emails
An email template per stage: "we received your request", "your request is solved"...
Customer Ratings
When a ticket is closed, the customer rates the support with a smiley and a comment.
Customer Portal
Customers see the tickets of their company, reply, add files, close a ticket and rate it.
Merge Tickets
Two emails about the same problem? Merge them: messages, files and followers move to one ticket.
Canned Responses
The canned responses of Discuss work in the chatter of tickets: type :: and pick an answer.
Reports
Open and closed tickets per team, time to first response and to close, SLA success rate, ratings.
