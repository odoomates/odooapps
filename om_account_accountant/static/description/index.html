Upgrading from an earlier 19 release

This release adds modules and changes several of the others, so they are upgraded together.

1Back up the database before anything else.
2Replace all the Odoo Mates accounting modules with this release, not only this app: the new ones (reconciliation, statement import, cheque printing, cash forecast, currency rates and revaluation, and the dashboards) must be in your addons path too.
3Restart Odoo, then Update Apps List in the Apps menu, in developer mode.
4Upgrade all the modules of the suite that are installed, together: Odoo upgrades a module and the ones depending on it, not the modules it depends on, so upgrading Odoo 19 Accounting Community alone is not enough. The new modules install themselves with it. From the command line: -u accounting_pdf_reports,om_account_accountant,om_account_asset,om_account_budget,om_account_daily_reports,om_account_followup,om_fiscal_year,om_recurring_payments (or -u all).

Your data is kept: the upgrade only adds to it. Nothing new switches itself on either: the automatic currency rates, the automatic reconciliation and the blocking over the credit limit stay off until you set them up.

Bank reconciliation statement

What the bank says, against what the books say

A payment sits in an outstanding account until a bank statement clears it, so the ledger balance and the money you actually hold are rarely the same number. This statement prints the difference, and the items behind it.

✓  Balance per bank, plus uncleared receipts, less uncleared payments, giving the cash position per books.
✓  A schedule of every uncleared item, with its date, reference, partner and amount.
✓  Bank items still sitting in the suspense account, listed separately.
✓  One section per bank journal, drawn up as at any date.
✓  Says so when a journal has no outstanding account set, instead of printing a wrong total.

The reconciliation, and the uncleared items behind it.
