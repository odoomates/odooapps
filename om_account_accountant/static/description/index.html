Features

A closer look

The main screens of the suite, in a few lines each.

The application

An accounting app, not an invoicing one

✓  Journals, Bank and Cash, Reconciliation, Ledgers and Closing menus.
✓  Journal Items for every accounting user, with a Reconcile action.
✓  Accountant, Advisor and Auditor groups; account tags and payment methods.
✓  Anglo-Saxon accounting, In Payment status and customer credit limit.
Banknew

Bank reconciliation, the way it should work

✓  Suggestions ranked by amount, reference, partner and direction.
✓  Match one item, several, or part of an amount; write off the difference.
✓  Reconciliation models and automatic reconciliation of new transactions.
✓  Match Journal Items for the open items outside the bank.
Bankrebuilt

Import the statements of your bank

✓  CAMT.053, MT940, OFX, QFX and QIF.
✓  CSV or Excel through a column map of your bank, kept for next time.
✓  Journal found from the account number; duplicates skipped.
✓  The file stays attached to the statement.
Vendor billsnew

Upload the bill. Check it. Post it.

✓  Vendor recognised by tax number, bank account, email or name.
✓  Number, dates, currency, taxes, totals and lines read and cross-checked.
✓  PDFs, scans and photos read on your server: no external service, no cost.
✓  Learns each vendor from the bills you post; never posts on its own.
Assets

Asset management with a depreciation board

✓  Linear, degressive, or degressive then linear, with prorata temporis.
✓  Assets created from vendor bill lines when the bill is posted.
✓  Modify, pause, sell or dispose of an asset, in part or in full.
✓  Depreciation Schedule and Assets Analysis.
Budgets

What was planned, and what was spent

✓  Budgets by budgetary position, analytic account or both.
✓  Actual, theoretical and achieved amounts on every line.
✓  Warning or block on documents that would go over budget.
✓  Copy to the next period, split by month or quarter.
Follow-ups

Get paid: customer follow-up management

✓  Who to remind today, at which level and for how much.
✓  Follow-up plans with emails, letters and manual actions per level.
✓  Promises to pay, disputes and excluded customers left out.
✓  Customer Statement PDF with ageing and bank details.
Cash forecastnew

Know what the bank balance will be

✓  1 to 60 weeks or months from the date of your choice.
✓  Open invoices and bills on their due date, payments in transit.
✓  Salaries, rent and taxes entered once or repeated.
✓  A warning when the balance goes negative.
Chequesnew

Print the cheques of any bank

✓  Every field placed in millimetres, calibrated with a test print.
✓  Amount in words, in another language or twice for bilingual cheques.
✓  MICR line in E-13B, US and Canadian layouts.
✓  Payment stubs; formats for the US, Canada, UK, Gulf, India and more.
Currenciesnew

Rates that update themselves

✓  ECB, Frankfurter, Open Exchange Rates, ExchangeRate-API or 36 central banks.
✓  Daily, weekly or monthly per company, with a backup source.
✓  A rate jumping more than 20% is held back and reported.
✓  Revaluation at the closing rate, reversed next period.
Closing

Fiscal years, period closing and lock dates

✓  Year-end closing entry to retained earnings; reopen a closed year.
✓  Period checklist: drafts, unreconciled items, duplicates, negative banks.
✓  Manual closing tasks marked done one by one.
✓  All lock dates set from one wizard.
Dashboardsnew

Eight accounting dashboards

✓  Overview, Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, Receivables and Payables.
✓  Bank and Cash, Assets, Budgets, Follow-ups and Cash Forecast.
✓  Compared with last year or the previous period.
✓  OM.ACCOUNT.BALANCE formula for your own spreadsheets.

How to install it

1Copy the module to a folder of your own modules, e.g. /opt/odoo/custom-addons, and add that folder to addons_path in odoo.conf.
2Restart Odoo.
3Activate the developer mode, then Apps → Update Apps List.
4Clear the Apps filter, search Odoo 20 Accounting Community and install it.
Do not use Apps → Import Module. It is meant for data modules without Python, and leaves these modules half loaded, ending on a missing field or view error.
Still failing? Write to odoomates@gmail.com with your Odoo version and the error.

Questions we get

Do I need Odoo Enterprise?
No. Everything on this page runs on the Community Edition.
Can I install only a part of it?
Yes. Each feature is a module of its own; this app installs them together and arranges their menus.
How do I use it in my language?
Add the language in Settings > Languages and pick it in your preferences. If a term reads wrong for an accountant of your country, tell us.
How do I skip In Payment and go straight to Paid?
Watch the answer on YouTube.
