New in Odoo 20

What this release adds

Seven capabilities written for Odoo 20 and one rewritten from scratch. All but the last are installed and arranged by this app. Everything below this section was already part of the suite and has been carried over.

Accounting DashboardsNew

Eight spreadsheet dashboards in the Dashboards app: overview, profit and loss, balance sheet, receivables and payables, bank and cash, assets, budgets and follow-ups.

Bank ReconciliationNew

A reconciliation screen of its own, with ranked suggestions, manual counterparts, reconciliation models and automatic reconciliation.

Cash ForecastNew

The bank balance week by week or month by month, from the open invoices and bills, the payments in transit and the items you expect.

Cheque PrintingNew

Configurable cheque formats for the cheques of any bank, positioned in millimetres, with payment stubs, a MICR line and a test print.

Automatic Currency RatesNew

Rates updated daily, weekly or monthly from the ECB, Frankfurter, Open Exchange Rates or ExchangeRate-API, with a backup source.

Currency RevaluationNew

The open foreign currency balances restated at the closing rate, as one adjustment entry reversed at the start of the next period.

Budget Control of PurchasesNewSeparate module

A Committed column on every budget line showing what the confirmed purchase orders have already promised and not yet billed, and a warning — or a block — on the purchase orders that would go over budget. This one is not installed by this app: it is the module Odoo 20 Budget Management - Purchase (om_account_budget_purchase), free on the Odoo Apps store like the rest of the suite. Download it into your addons path and it installs itself as soon as Purchase and the budgets are both there, or install it by hand from the Apps list.

Bank Statement ImportRebuilt

Rewritten for 20.0: CAMT.053, MT940, OFX, QFX, QIF, and CSV or Excel read through a column map of your own bank.

The application

An accounting application, not an invoicing one

The Invoicing application becomes Accounting, and the menus of every installed app are arranged where an accountant looks for them: the daily work under Accounting, the reports under Reporting, the settings under Configuration.

✓  A Journals menu with the sales, purchase, bank and cash, and miscellaneous entries.
✓  A Bank and Cash menu with the bank statements and the cash registers.
✓  Reconciliation, Ledgers, Closing and Management sections gathered in one place.
✓  Journal Items available to every accounting user, with a Reconcile action.
✓  Account Tags and, in developer mode, the Payment Methods of the journals.
✓  Accountant, Advisor and Auditor groups, with an Advisor being an Accountant as well.

The accounting dashboard: journals, what is left to reconcile and the cheques to print.

Assets

Asset management with a depreciation board

Record an asset, preview its depreciation, confirm it, and let the entries be posted on their date. Modify, pause, sell or dispose of it at any time.

✓  Asset categories holding the accounts, the journal and the depreciation method.
✓  Linear, degressive, or degressive then linear, over a number of entries, up to a date or at a rate.
✓  Prorata temporis, a first depreciation date of your own, and the depreciation already booked.
✓  Assets created automatically from the lines of a vendor bill when it is posted.
✓  Modify a running asset from a date, pause and resume it, sell or dispose of it in part or in full.
✓  Depreciation Schedule report and an Assets Analysis in graph and pivot views.

Budgets

Budgets, with what was planned and what was spent

Plan by budgetary position, by analytic account or by both, and follow on every line the actual amount, the theoretical amount expected today and the achievement.

✓  Draft, confirmed, validated, done and cancelled, with the changes locked once it is confirmed.
✓  Expense and revenue lines, spread over the period or expected in full on a planned date.
✓  An Over Budget filter, and a warning banner on the vendor bills that would go over a line.
✓  Documents over budget can be blocked for everyone but the accounting managers.
✓  With Purchase installed, a Committed column shows what the confirmed purchase orders have already promised on each expense line but not billed yet.
✓  Purchase orders going over budget warn the buyer before confirmation, and are blocked too when the blocking option is on.
✓  Copy a budget to the next period, or split its lines by month or by quarter.
✓  A Budget vs Actual PDF and a budget analysis in pivot, list and graph views.
Optional add-on · new in 20.0

Budget control of purchase orders

The two points above come from a module of their own that this app does not install for you: Odoo 20 Budget Management - Purchase (om_account_budget_purchase). It is free on the Odoo Apps store, like every module of this suite.

Download it, drop it in your addons path, then update the apps list: it installs itself as soon as Purchase and the budgets are both installed, or you can install it by hand from Apps. Without it the budgets work exactly as described above, counting the vendor bills only.

BankNew in 20.0

Bank reconciliation, the way it should work

A screen of its own for the bank and cash transactions: pick a journal, see the open items that are likely to be the counterpart, and validate.

✓  Suggestions ranked by amount, reference, partner and direction.
✓  Match one item, several items or a part of an amount; add a counterpart by hand with its taxes.
✓  Apply a reconciliation model, or let the automatic reconciliation treat the new transactions.
✓  Undo a reconciliation, mark a transaction to check, or leave a note for a colleague.
✓  Match Journal Items: the open items of the reconcilable accounts, grouped by account and partner.
✓  Write off a difference on the journal, account, date and label of your choice.

The bank reconciliation screen.

Match Journal Items, outside the bank.

Import the statements of your bank

CAMT.053 (ISO 20022), MT940 (SWIFT), OFX or QFX, QIF, and CSV or Excel with a map of the columns of your bank, kept for the next import. The journal is recognised from the account number in the file, the transactions already imported are skipped, and the file stays attached to the statement.

Import Statement.

ChequesNew in 20.0

Print the cheques of any bank

Place every printed field at an exact position in millimetres, on a cheque book leaf or on a voucher cheque, and calibrate it with a test print.

✓  A4, US Letter or a custom paper size, with the cheque on top, in the middle or at the bottom.
✓  Width, height, font size, bold, alignment, spacing, rotation and border for every field.
✓  Amount in words in capitals, in another language, or twice for bilingual cheques.
✓  Cents in words or as a fraction, and amounts surrounded by stars.
✓  A MICR line in E-13B for blank cheque paper, in the US (ANSI X9) or Canadian (CPA-005) layout.
✓  One or two payment stubs listing the bills paid, with VOID on the pages that follow.
✓  Formats installed for the US, Canada, the UK and Ireland, the Gulf, Australia, New Zealand, India.

A cheque format: every field placed in millimetres from the top left corner.

Closing

Fiscal years, period closing and lock dates

Close a month with a checklist, close a year with an entry, and lock what must not move any more.

✓  Fiscal years per company, shorter or longer than a year when the option is enabled.

✓  A period closing checklist: draft entries, transactions to reconcile, payments not matched, negative bank balances and duplicate bills, refreshed on demand.
✓  Manual tasks copied from the closing tasks, marked done one by one.
✓  Choose what a closing locks: nothing, the sales and purchases, the taxes, or everything.

Journal items

The entries, for every accounting user

The Journal Items menu is moved to the top of its section and opened to every accounting user, with a Reconcile action for the accountants. The In Payment status is kept on the invoices, so a registered payment does not mark an invoice paid before the payment is reconciled.

Journal Items.

Anglo-Saxon Accounting, the customer credit limit and the fiscal year options are added to the accounting settings. The payment account of each payment method is shown on the journal form.
