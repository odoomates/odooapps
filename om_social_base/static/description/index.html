Social Inbox

Every chat with your customers in Discuss: one conversation per customer, shared by the team of the account, with the status of each message, receipts, reactions and replies, and the document it speaks about. WhatsApp today, on a base made for more messaging channels.

What your team gets

One conversation per customer
A Social Inbox tab in the messaging menu and in Discuss. The users of the account are its members; a new message opens its chat window; what they write there goes out on the channel.
Replies on the right document
An answer goes to the chatter of the document of the message it quotes, or of the last one sent to that customer, and the conversation opens that document from its header.
Status you can trust
Queued, sent, delivered, read or failed, with ticks on each message. A failed message is in the failures of the messaging menu and goes again in one click.
Receipts and reactions
Reading a conversation marks the messages read for the customer, a voice message played is marked played, and reactions go both ways, with the providers that can.
Customers Odoo does not know
An unknown customer opens a conversation named by their address and profile name, with Create Contact. Or keep their chats out of Odoo, per account.
Identities per channel
The user ID and username a channel gives a customer find their contact, even when their number is not shared, and show on the contact.

Every message in one list

Social Inbox > Messages lists what was sent and received from every account, with filters for the failed and the queued ones.

Accounts for your teams

An account is a number or a handle, its channel and provider, and the users who answer from it. Set its reply window, whether it keeps the chats of unknown customers, and whether it shows their profile pictures.

On every contact

The conversations of a contact are one click away, and their identities in the channels show under their phone.

For developers

A channel module adds its key to the channel of om.social.account; a provider module adds its key to the provider and implements _om_social_send_<provider>, and optionally _om_social_react_<provider>, _om_social_receipt_<provider> and _om_social_picture_<provider>. Incoming messages go to _om_social_receive, statuses to _om_social_update_status, reactions to _om_social_receive_reaction. The README of the module has the details.
