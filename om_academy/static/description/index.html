A module to learn with, for beginners first

The Academy is for learning: it is not an app to run your business. If you are a beginner to coding, or to Odoo, install it on a test database and try it out: it starts from an empty folder, and explains every word on the way.

📖 A story, not a manual
Each step answers what the step before left open: a table, then who may see it, then a door to it...
💬 Every new word explained
Model, access right, action, menu, view, domain: in plain words, the first time you meet them.
🔒 One step at a time
Next opens once the step is built: nothing skipped, nothing missed.
🌳 Watch the code grow
The whole module beside each step: click a file, see what the step added.
🎲 Warm-up quiz
Rounds of 8 questions, a new set each time, on Odoo and on what you built. Your scores are kept.
🔍 Find it
The browser console, the developer mode, a model name, an id, an external id: what they are, then find them.
Two ways to learn

Pick yours, switch any time

⚡ Build it live

No editor, no setup. Each step shows its code, then builds the same thing in your database: you see it appear at once.

✓  What just happened: the records Odoo stores, explained
✓  Undo any step, or start the course over
✓  Export as app: the very module, as a zip

💻 Code it

Write the files in your own editor. Install or upgrade from the screen, then let the Academy check your module.

✓  Checked: models, fields, views, menus, access rights
✓  Precise messages: "does not depend on state: add it to @api.depends"
✓  Stuck? Write it for me, or compare with the reference
🧪 Fresh out of the oven: Code it is not tested as much as Build it live yet. Something may break here and there... and that is your chance: fix it for us, and open a pull request.

The courses

Start with your first app, then go further

BEGINNER · 20 MIN · 10 STEPS

Build your first app

From an empty folder to a real app: models, access rights, menus, views, a link, buttons, a computed field. Pick what it is about: a Library, Car Rental, a Hotel or Tool Lending. Then go a little more: conditions, a chatter, tracking.
BEGINNER · 15 MIN · 6 STEPS

Extend Odoo: members in Contacts

Add fields to Odoo's contacts with _inherit, put them in Odoo's own form, list and search with XPath, and compute a value.
INTERMEDIATE · 20 MIN · 5 STEPS

Organize with a Kanban board

Cards in columns, dragged from stage to stage: stages loaded as data, a link, and the Kanban view of Odoo 20, with colors and priority stars.
INTERMEDIATE · 15 MIN · 5 STEPS

Print a PDF report

A contact card printed from any contact, as Odoo prints its invoices: a report in the Print menu, a QWeb template, a paper format.
INTERMEDIATE · 15 MIN · 7 STEPS

Secure an app, the Odoo 20 way

Groups under a privilege, access rights per group, a domain so each user sees only their own records, menus and fields for managers only.
INTERMEDIATE · 35 MIN · 5 STEPS · NEW

Your first Owl components

JavaScript in Odoo, in your editor: a screen of your own, its state, a component in it, data from the server with the ORM, notifications and actions. The Owl of Odoo 20.
