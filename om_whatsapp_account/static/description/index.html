What it does

Through the WhatsApp connector of Odoo Mates, with the provider of your choice.

Invoices
The PDF and the link to view and pay online.
Credit Notes
Their own template, with the PDF.
Payment Receipts
The receipt of a customer payment.
Automatic
On posting and on confirmation, once.
From the List
Several invoices in one click.
Templates
Edit the texts, or pick your own.
Any Provider
Meta, WhatsApp Web, QR or Click to Chat.
Safe
Opt-outs skipped; a failure never stops posting.

Send by WhatsApp
A button on posted customer invoices and credit notes opens the composer with the template.
✓  The invoice PDF attached, for the providers that send files.
✓  The link to view and pay the invoice on the portal.
✓  The number of the customer, ready to send.
Payment receipts
A confirmed customer payment sends its receipt, by hand or by itself.
✓  The payment receipt PDF attached.
✓  Kept in the chatter of the payment, with its status.
✓  Sent once, when the payment is confirmed.
Settings
Invoicing > Configuration > Settings > WhatsApp.
✓  Send each invoice and credit note when it is posted.
✓  Send each payment receipt when it is confirmed.
✓  Choose the templates and the account to send from.

Works with the WhatsApp Connector

Needs the base; add one provider. Messages go by themselves with Meta and QR; WhatsApp Web sends them from the browser of the user, Click to Chat opens WhatsApp with the text filled in. With Meta, submit the templates for approval first.

WhatsApp Connector Base
Templates, composer and message log: needed by this app.
WhatsApp Connector Meta
The official Cloud API: sends by itself, approved templates.
WhatsApp Connector Web
Texts and PDFs from WhatsApp Web in your browser.
WhatsApp Connector QR
Your WhatsApp number linked by a QR code, sending by itself.
