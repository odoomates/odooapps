AI Vendor Bill Reading

Adds an AI engine to Vendor Bill OCR: your own AI model reads the uploaded bills, PDFs from their text, scans and photos with vision. The bill stays a draft for you to check.

Your AI, your key
Built on the free AI Engine (om_ai): the requests go from your Odoo server straight to the provider you choose, with your key, or to a model server on your own machine. Nothing goes through Odoo Mates. Every call is in the AI usage log, within your daily limits.

Read by your AI model

The vendor is recognized by its tax number, bank account, email or name, the account and the taxes come from what earlier bills taught. Lines are kept only when they add up to the untaxed amount.

Photos too

A photo of a bill goes to the vision model. The confidence and the flags of the AI are posted on the bill, and the fields to check are shown.

One setting

Choose the engine: on your server, or with your AI model, with a fallback on the server reading when the AI fails.
Good to know
  • Needs Vendor Bill OCR and AI Document Reading (installed together automatically).
  • Usage is logged under the feature "extract". Bills are never posted automatically.
