Your AI, your key
Built on the free AI Engine (om_ai): the requests go from your Odoo server straight to the provider you choose, with your key, or to a model server on your own machine. Nothing goes through Odoo Mates. Every call is in the AI usage log, within your daily limits.

Answers in the chat window

Visitors chat as usual. The agent answers from your knowledge in their language, looks up products with their current price and stock, and takes their contact details for a quote. Answers come in the background with a typing indicator.

Your team takes over

A visitor asking for a person, a question the agent cannot answer, or the answer limit of the chat: the chat goes to an online operator of the channel, who sees the whole conversation. The agent then stops answering in that chat.

Safety

Under control

Hand-over rules, limits, and no invented prices.

No invented price
An amount the tools or the knowledge did not give is never sent: the chat goes to your team instead.
Hand-over
On request (your words), when the agent is not sure, within operator hours.
Limits
Answers per chat, messages per visitor per hour, characters per message.
Every answer logged
With the knowledge parts and the tools used, and the AI usage.

Operator hours

Hand over within your hours only, in your time zone. Outside them, or when nobody is online, the visitor gets your message and the agent can still take their email for a lead or a ticket.

On your channels

Choose the agent on a live chat channel: it answers always, or only when no operator is available.

Answers

Each question with its answer, its state (answered, handed over, failed) and what the agent used.
Good to know
  • You need the AI Engine (om_ai) with a provider and default models set up.
  • Leads need CRM, tickets need Odoo Mates Helpdesk, prices and stock need the sales and inventory apps: the tools appear when the apps are installed.
  • Prices are the list prices of the products.
  • Usage is logged under the feature "livechat". Check the answers with your own provider and model.
