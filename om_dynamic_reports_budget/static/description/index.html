Budgets in the Reports

What was planned next to what was booked, in the dynamic Profit and Loss.

Budget columnAchievement %Installs by itself
✓  Pick one or more budgets on the Profit and Loss: a Budget column next to the balance.
✓  The achievement: the balance as a percentage of the budget, per account, section and in total.
✓  Lines spread evenly count for their share of the period; lines at a date when their date is in it.
✓  A budgetary position of several accounts is spread over them as they booked, so the sections add up.
