=======================
Documents - Accounting
=======================

Bridge between Documents and Accounting.

* Create a vendor bill, a customer invoice or a credit note from a document: the
  file of the document becomes the main attachment of the new invoice, read like
  a file uploaded to the journal (e-invoices, and the PDFs and scans when a
  module reads them, e.g. Vendor Bill OCR). The document is then linked to the
  invoice.
* From the form of a document (Create Bill), from the selected documents, or
  with a workflow action of the folder (Vendor Bill, Vendor Credit Note,
  Customer Invoice, Customer Credit Note, with a journal of your choice).
* The files of the invoices and the bills are kept in an Accounting folder,
  tagged by kind of invoice, with the partner as their contact (a setting per
  company, in Documents > Configuration > Settings).
* A Documents smart button on the invoices.
