Field Service - Stock

The parts used on the jobs, taken from the van of the technician, and the serial numbers installed at the customer site.

Add-on of Field Service, installed with Inventory.

Parts without paperwork

The technician picks the parts on the phone; the stock follows when the job is done.

Van Stock
A stock location per technician, created in one click.
Parts
Product, quantity, lot or serial number, from the van or a warehouse.
Stock Moves
A validated transfer to the customer when the job is done.
Serial Numbers
Only the serial numbers in stock in the van are offered.
Equipment at Site
The serial numbers installed are kept on the customer site.
Service History
The next jobs pick the equipment serviced.
Replenish
An internal transfer with the parts used on the last jobs.
On the Report
The parts are printed on the job report.

Screens from a test database with the demo data.

Parts on the phone
Search a product or scan its barcode, see what is in the van, pick the serial number.
✓  Products in the van first, with their quantity.
✓  Serial numbers in stock only.
✓  Removed as long as the job is not done.
Parts on the job
The parts of the job, where they come from and their status.
✓  Taken out of stock when the job is done.
✓  A transfer linked to the job.
✓  Installed at the site: recorded on the customer.
Equipment at the sites
What is installed where, with its serial number and installation job.
✓  Created from the parts installed.
✓  Or recorded by hand.
✓  The jobs of each piece of equipment.
Van stock
The stock of every van, by technician.
✓  Replenish from the employee: the parts used are proposed.
✓  Internal transfer from the warehouse.
✓  Standard Inventory reports work on it.
Parts on the report
The customer sees the parts and serial numbers used.
✓  Product, lot or serial number, quantity.
✓  In the PDF sent when the job is done.
✓  And in the customer portal.
