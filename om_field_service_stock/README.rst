=======================
Field Service - Stock
=======================

Parts used on field service jobs, with Inventory.

* **Van stock**: a stock location per technician (*Create Van* on the
  employee), its stock in *Field Service > Inventory > Van Stock*.
* **Parts** on the job and on the phone screen: product, quantity, lot or
  serial number, taken from the van of the technician or from a warehouse.
  The phone shows what is in the van first and the serial numbers available.
* **Done**: the parts leave their location for the customer in a validated
  transfer linked to the job.
* **Equipment at the site**: the serial numbers installed are recorded on the
  customer site; the next jobs pick the equipment serviced.
* **Replenish the van**: an internal transfer from the warehouse with the parts
  used on the jobs of the last days.
* The parts are printed on the job report.
