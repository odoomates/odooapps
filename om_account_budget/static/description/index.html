Features

A closer look

Budgets

What was planned, and what it has cost so far

✓  One line per budgetary position, analytic account, or both.
✓  Expense and revenue lines, spread over the period or expected in full on a date.
✓  Draft, confirmed, validated and done, with the lines locked once confirmed.
✓  Copy to the next period, or split the lines by month or by quarter.
✓  Audit a line to open the journal items or analytic items behind it.
Positions

Group the accounts the way you steer the business

✓  As many positions as you need, each with its own accounts.
✓  Expense or revenue positions, so over budget means the right thing.
✓  Combine a position with an analytic account to budget a project or a department.
Report

Budget against actual, printed for the file

✓  A Budget vs Actual PDF from the budget.
✓  Expenses and revenues: planned, expected to date, actual, remaining and achieved.
✓  A Budget Report button on the budget opens its lines in the analysis.
Control

Know before the money is spent, not after

✓  A warning banner on draft vendor bills that would push a line over its plan.
✓  Block those bills for everyone but the accounting managers.
✓  The responsible is warned at a threshold you set, then when a line goes over.
✓  An Over Budget filter, and pivot and graph views by position, account or period.
How it works

In three steps

1
Define the positions
Group the expense and revenue accounts you want to follow.
2
Plan the budget
Add a line per position or analytic account, then confirm and approve.
3
Follow it
Actuals come from the posted entries; warnings come before the overspend.

Install

Get started

How to install it

1Copy the module to a folder of your own modules, e.g. /opt/odoo/custom-addons, and add that folder to addons_path in odoo.conf.
2Restart Odoo.
3Activate the developer mode, then Apps → Update Apps List.
4Clear the Apps filter, search om_account_budget and install it.
Do not use Apps → Import Module. It is meant for data modules without Python, and leaves this module half loaded.

The whole suite, in one install

Odoo 20 Accounting installs this module with every other one of the suite, the accounting menus and the dashboards, and comes with a user guide for the person keeping the books.

Questions we get

Can it control purchase orders as well?
Yes, with the free om_account_budget_purchase module: warnings and blocks on purchase orders, and the amounts they commit on the budget lines. It installs on its own when Purchase is there.
Where do the actual amounts come from?
From the posted journal items on the accounts of the position, or from the analytic items when the line has an analytic account.
Do I need the full accounting app?
No. Budget Management works on its own. For complete accounting, install om_account_accountant, which brings it together with the rest of the suite.
Do I need Odoo Enterprise?
No. Everything on this page runs on the Community Edition.
