Budgets

What was planned, and what it has cost so far

A budget covers a period and holds one line per budgetary position or analytic account. Each line shows the planned amount, the theoretical amount expected by today, the actual amount posted and the achievement.

  • ✓  Draft, confirmed, validated, done and cancelled, with a responsible for each budget.
  • ✓  Expenses planned as negative amounts and revenues as positive ones, spread over the period or expected in full on a paid date.

A budget with its lines and the amounts achieved.

Positions

Group the accounts the way you steer the business

A budgetary position is a named group of accounts, and it is what a budget line is measured against.

  • ✓  As many positions as you need, each with its own accounts.
  • ✓  Combine a position with an analytic account to budget a project or a department.

Budgetary positions and their accounts.

Reporting

Budget against actual, analysed

A budget analysis in pivot, list and graph views, and the Budgets dashboard of the Dashboards app.

  • ✓  By position, by analytic account or by period.
  • ✓  Exported to a spreadsheet from the pivot.

Budget analysis.

Budget against actual in the Profit and Loss, free

Install Dynamic Accounting Reports (om_dynamic_reports) with this module and the budgets show up in its Profit and Loss by themselves (om_dynamic_reports_budget).

✓  A Budget column next to the balance of every account, for the budgets you pick.
✓  Achievement %: what was booked as a share of what was planned.
✓  A budget line counts for its share of the days of the period.
✓  A budgetary position of several accounts spread over them as they booked.
✓  Export to PDF, Excel and CSV like every dynamic report.
