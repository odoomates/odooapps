Where

One more entry in the Print menu

Open a journal entry, an invoice, a bill or a payment entry, click Print, then Journal Entries. Select several entries in the list to print them in one go.

The PDF

The entry, line by line

On your company letterhead: the entry number, its journal, date, partner and reference, then the lines and the totals of debit and credit.

✓Account, date, partner and label of each line.
✓The analytic account of each line.
✓Debit and credit in the currency of the line, and their totals.

Install

Install Print Journal Entries: it only needs Invoicing or Accounting. Nothing to configure.

Already using Accounting PDF Reports? This print came from it, and that module now installs this one for you: nothing changes for your users.
