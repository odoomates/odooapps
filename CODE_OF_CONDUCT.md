# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Odoo Mates and everyone who takes part in this project (in issues, pull requests,
discussions and reviews) pledge to make it a harassment-free experience for
everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The points below apply it to this repository.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, with users and with maintainers. Most people here
  give their time for free.
- Assume good intent. Many contributors write in a second language.
- Keep to the subject: one issue for one problem, comments that move it forward.
- Accept constructive criticism of your code, and give it kindly.
- Credit the work of others.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, harassment, personal or political attacks, and sexualised language
  or images.
- Publishing someone's private information (email, address, customer data)
  without their permission.
- Spam: advertising, unrelated links, "+1" or "any update?" comments in bulk,
  and mass automated issues or pull requests.
- Demanding support or deadlines. Support and customisation are offered
  separately, see [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).

## Scope

This code applies in every space of the project: the repository, its issues,
pull requests and discussions, and anywhere a person represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **odoomates@gmail.com**. Every report is read
and kept confidential.

The maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, close issues and pull
requests, and temporarily or permanently block anyone who breaks this code,
following the
[enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
a correction, a warning, a temporary ban, then a permanent ban.
