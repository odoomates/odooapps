The statuses

What Release to Pay tells you

Yes
Every line linked to a purchase order is received (or ordered, for a 2-way product) and priced within the tolerance.
No
The bill waits: more is billed than received so far, or the bill has no purchase order and the rule asks for a manual release.
Exception
More is billed than ordered, or a price is above the purchase order beyond the tolerance.
Forced
A manager set Yes or No by hand, with a reason; set it back to automatic at any time.

Settings

FAQ

Questions

Which apps does it need?
Purchase and Inventory (for the receipts) with Invoicing or Accounting. Batch payments are checked too when Odoo Mates Batch Payments is installed.
What about services?
A service billed on received quantities waits for the received quantity set on the purchase order line. A product or service billed on ordered quantities is matched with the order only, unless you ask for the receipt too.
Are lines without purchase order a problem?
On a bill with purchase order lines, extra lines such as freight are left out of the match. A bill with no purchase order line at all follows the rule of the settings.
Does it change how bills are created?
No. Create bills as usual, from the order or with the Auto-Complete of the bill; the match is computed from the purchase order lines linked to the bill lines.
