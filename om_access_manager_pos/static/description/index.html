User Access Manager for POS

The Point of Sale rules of POS Access Control on the access profiles of the User Access Manager.
One profile for every cashier: refunds, discounts, prices, cash, closing, customers, payment methods.

The Cashiers profile: what its users may not do in the shop.

What it controls

Everything POS Access Control does, per profile

Tick the rules once on the profile: every user of it gets them.

↩

Refunds

The Refund button goes, and refunds sent anyway are refused.
%

Discounts

No discount at all, or a maximum per line: a higher one is brought down to it.
🏷

Prices

No price change, or a maximum cut below the pricelist, checked before paying.
📈

Never below cost

A price or a discount that sells for less than the cost is refused.
💵

Cash in and out

Cash In/Out leaves the menu and the closing popup; cash moves sent anyway are refused.
🔒

Closing the register

Close Register leaves the menu; closing sent anyway is refused.
👤

Customers

No creating or editing customers from the shop. Choosing one stays possible.
➖

Lowering quantities

Once a product is on the order, it cannot be removed or lowered: a manager has to.
💳

Payment methods

Only the methods allowed are shown, and orders paid otherwise are refused.
Combined

Profiles and users together

Rules on the profile and rules on the user both apply: the strictest wins. Several profiles combine as the other rules of the User Access Manager.

✓  A restriction of every additive profile, or of any override one.
✓  The loosest limit of the additive profiles, tightened by override ones.
✓  Rules set on the user themselves apply too.
✓  Preview as User shows the rest of their access.

Questions we get

Is it really free?
Yes, all of it, under LGPL-3.

What do I need?
The User Access Manager and the Point of Sale. Once both are installed, it installs by itself, with POS Access Control.

Employees with a badge or a PIN?
Add User Access Manager for POS Employees to list them on profiles.

Is it kept in the history of the profile?
Yes: every change is in the chatter and in the versions of the profile.
