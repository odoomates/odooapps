Inter-Company Invoices and Bills

One company of the group invoices another: the vendor bill is there already, with its taxes, accounts and currency, and the PDF of the invoice. And a report shows what each company owes the others, as both sides book it.

At a glance

Enter it once, in the company that issues it

Rules between the companies of the group decide which documents are created in the other company, in draft or posted, and how they are filled in there.

Invoice becomes Bill
A posted customer invoice to a group company becomes its vendor bill; a credit note its refund. Vendor bills can become customer invoices too.
Inter-Company User
Documents are created as the user of the rule: the person posting the invoice needs no access to the other company.
Taxes Mapped
The sales tax becomes the purchase tax with the same rate, through the fiscal position of the partner, or as your mapping table says.
Accounts and Products
Accounts from the products of the receiving company or by code; shared products kept, others mapped.
Currencies
The bill in the currency of the invoice, or converted to the currency of the receiving company at the invoice date.
Balances and Differences
What each company owes the others, from both sides, with the differences to clear before closing.
Features

A closer look

Rules

One rule per pair of companies

✓  One way or both ways, customer invoices to vendor bills and vendor bills to customer invoices.
✓  Created in draft for a check, or posted at once; reset or cancel followed, or warned.
✓  Journals of each company, the user the documents are created as, the PDF attached.
Issuing company

Post the invoice as usual

✓  A user of the issuing company only: the bill is created in the other company all the same.
✓  A smart button shows the bill and its company; the chatter links both.
Receiving company

The vendor bill, with the invoice PDF

✓  Vendor, bill reference, payment reference and due date from the invoice.
✓  Purchase taxes of the receiving company, the PDF of the invoice beside the bill.
Credit notes

Refunds follow, linked with their bill

✓  A credit note becomes a vendor refund linked with the bill it refunds.
✓  Here in another currency: converted at the rate of the invoice date.
Mapping

Taxes your way

✓  Account, tax and product mapping tables, used in both directions.
✓  Without a mapping: same rate, or the taxes of the product, and the fiscal position.
Changes

Reset or cancelled: nothing gets lost

✓  Warn: the bill shows what happened to its invoice, until dismissed.
✓  Follow: the bill is reset or cancelled too, unless it is paid.
Changes

Told at once

✓  The person resetting the invoice is told what happened to the bill.
✓  Posting the invoice again updates a bill still in draft.
Report

Inter-company balances, from both sides

✓  Per company and other company: what is owed in our books, in their books, and the difference.
✓  Group by books and account, presentation currency, key figures, PDF and Excel export.
✓  Receivable and payable accounts, or only the accounts flagged Inter-Company.
Report

The journal items of both books

✓  A line opens the journal items behind it, of both companies: find the missing bill at once.
Settings

Where to find it

✓  Rules in Accounting, Sales and Purchase configuration, the report in Partner Reports.
Questions

Good to know

Do the users of one company need access to the other?
No. The rule names the user the documents are created as (OdooBot by default). Only that user needs access to both companies.
Can a bill be copied back into an invoice and loop?
No. A document created from another is never copied again.
What about sales and purchase orders?
Install Inter-Company Sales and Purchases: a sales order becomes the purchase order of the other company, and the invoice of the order becomes the bill of that purchase order.
Is netting included?
Not yet. The balance report shows what each company owes the others and the differences; settle them with your usual payments.
