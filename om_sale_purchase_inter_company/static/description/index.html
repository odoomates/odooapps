Inter-Company Sales and Purchases

One company of the group sells to another: confirming the sales order creates the purchase order of the other company, its delivery validates the receipt with the same serial numbers, and its invoice becomes the bill of the purchase order.

At a glance

The order is entered once

The rule between two companies decides which orders are created in the other company, how, and in which warehouse.

Sales to Purchase
Confirming a sales order to a group company creates its purchase order, as a request for quotation or confirmed.
Purchase to Sales
Confirming a purchase order to a group company creates its sales order, with its delivery.
Receipt with the Delivery
Validating the delivery validates the receipt of the other company with the same quantities, lots and serials.
Changes Followed
Quantities, added lines and cancellations are followed, with a warning when the other order is processed already.
Invoice to Bill
The invoice of the order becomes the bill of the purchase order, linked with its lines: billed once.
Your Settings
Warehouse and receipt type of each company, prices kept or from the pricelists, salesperson and buyer.
Features

A closer look

Rules

Sales and purchases on the inter-company rule

✓  Sales orders to purchase orders, purchase orders to sales orders, or both.
✓  Request for quotation or confirmed, prices kept, receipt validated with the delivery.
✓  Warehouse and receipt type of each company.
Selling company

Confirm the sales order

✓  The purchase order of the other company is created at once, shown by a smart button.
Buying company

The purchase order is there

✓  Vendor reference is the sales order, prices and quantities as sold, receipt planned.
Selling company

Validate the delivery

✓  Serial numbers reserved and delivered as usual.
Buying company

The receipt is validated with it

✓  Same quantities, same serial numbers, created in the buying company.
✓  What is not delivered stays in a backorder.
Changes

Processed already? Both orders are warned

✓  A quantity changed before the receipt changes the purchase order too.
✓  After the receipt or the bill, both orders get a warning instead.
Invoicing

The invoice becomes the bill of the order

✓  The bill is linked with the purchase order lines: billed quantities up to date, nothing billed twice.
The other way

A purchase order becomes a sales order

✓  The buying company orders first: its purchase order creates the sales order of the selling company.
✓  Prices converted to the currency of the sales order when needed.
Questions

Good to know

What does it need?
Inter-Company Invoices and Bills (installed with it), Sales and Purchase with Inventory.
A product of one company only?
Map it in the Product Mapping of the rule; a line without a product available in the other company is left out with a warning.
Who creates the orders?
The user of the rule, in the other company; the salesperson and buyer of the rule are set on the orders.
Are returns copied?
No. The receipt follows the deliveries of the selling company; returns are handled in each company.
