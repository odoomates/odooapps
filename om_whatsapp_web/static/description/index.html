Install, and use it. That is all.

It works with the WhatsApp you already use: your number, your chats, WhatsApp Web open in a tab of your browser. There is no account to open anywhere, and nothing to pay to anyone.

Install WhatsApp Connector Web; it brings the base module WhatsApp Connector Base with it.

Install the free Odoo Mates WhatsApp Connector from the Chrome Web Store (Firefox add-on coming soon), then click its icon on your Odoo and Allow this Odoo.

While WhatsApp Web is open, the extension brings the messages of your contacts into Odoo: each number gets its WhatsApp conversation in Discuss, shared by the users of the account, and you answer from there.

  • ✓  A WhatsApp tab in the messaging menu; the chat window opens when a message comes in.
  • ✓  Pictures, documents, stickers and voice messages come with their files; reactions show on the messages.
  • ✓  The ticks of WhatsApp: sent, delivered, read.
  • ✓  A number you do not know yet opens a conversation where you make the contact in one click.

Questions we get

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API, or Meta?
No. It sends through WhatsApp Web, open in your browser, as you would by hand. To send without a browser, there is WhatsApp Connector QR (om_whatsapp_device), from your server; WhatsApp Connector Meta (om_whatsapp), through the Meta Cloud API, is coming soon.

What must stay open?
Your browser, with WhatsApp Web linked to your phone. Its tab may stay in the background; it is opened when it is not. The replies come to Odoo while it is open, and those that came while it was closed when you open it again.

Are my private chats read into Odoo?
Groups and status never. By default, a number Odoo does not know opens a conversation, so that you can make the contact; uncheck Keep Chats of Unknown Numbers on the account, and only your contacts and the chats about your documents come to Odoo. The others never leave your browser.

And without the extension?
Send tells you: click the icon of the extension and Allow this Odoo, then Send again. Or open the chat in WhatsApp Web with the text filled in, and send it there yourself, without the files.

Can I send to many contacts at once?
No, and on purpose: each message is sent when you click Send. WhatsApp does not allow automated or bulk sending from WhatsApp Web, and bans the numbers that do it.

Can the quotation go when I confirm an order?
Yes, with an automation rule you set up: install the Automation Rules app of Odoo (the WhatsApp modules do not install it), then, in developer mode, go to Settings > Technical > Automation Rules and create a rule on Sales Order, triggered when the State is set to Sales Order, with an action of type Send WhatsApp and your template; its report goes as a PDF. It goes through your browser when you confirm one order yourself; orders confirmed several at once, or by the website, wait to be sent again from the chatter.

Which browsers?
Chrome, Edge and the other Chromium browsers, and Firefox, on a computer.
