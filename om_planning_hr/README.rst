=====================
Planning - Employees
=====================

Bridge between Planning and Employees, installed automatically with both.

Features
========

* Every employee is a planning resource with their working schedule, time
  zone, department and manager; all employees show on the Gantt.
* Planning roles and default role on the employee form, with a Planning
  smart button; a Planning button on departments.
* Slots get the department of their employee: "By Department" schedule,
  "My Department" and "My Team" filters, group by department.
* Schedules are emailed to the employee's user, or to their work email when
  they have no user, with a link to their own planning page and calendar
  feed.
* Claims of open shifts, switches and leave requests are approved by the
  employee's manager or department manager, besides the planning managers.
