Approvals

Put any button of any app under approval, without code: confirming a quotation above an amount, posting a vendor bill, validating a transfer. The record waits, locked, until the right people approve it, then the button runs by itself.

How it works

Click, approve, done

Nothing changes for the people who use the button: they click it as always. Odoo asks the approval in their place and finishes the job once it is given.

1
Someone clicks the button
Confirm on a quotation above $5,000. Instead of confirming it, Odoo asks why, with files if needed, and asks the approval. The quotation is locked.
2
The approvers decide
Each step in turn: the sales manager, then the director above $20,000. They get an activity, approve or refuse on the record, with a note.
3
The button runs by itself
Once the last approval comes, Confirm runs as the person who clicked it. A refusal shows on the record, with the reason.

Everything at a glance

Set up flow by flow, from simple to strict.

The flow
Any button of any app
Confirm, Post, Validate, Cancel, or a button of your own module, chosen in one click.
Amounts in a currency
Above an amount, in the currency of the flow; other currencies are converted first.
Steps in turn
Specific people, a group, or a person on the record, such as the salesperson or team leader.
One, all, or some
A step is approved by any one, by all, or by two of three. The record shows how many so far.
While it waits
Locked while it waits
Read only from the request until the button has run, or until an approval manager unlocks it.
Notes and files
The requester says why, with files; the approvers write why they approve or refuse.
Reminders and escalation
Approvers are reminded after some days; a step waiting too long goes to other people too.
Delegation
An approver away names a substitute for the dates, who decides on their behalf.
Built for control
Locked for real
Refused from the form, a list, an import and the API, for everyone; the chatter still works.
External APIs too
JSON-2, XML-RPC and JSON-RPC ask for the approval instead of running the button.
A full trail
Who asked, why, who approved or refused each step, when and on whose behalf.
Test before you trust
Test on a Record shows what a flow would do for any record and requester.

Designer

Design it as a chain of cards

A flow reads from top to bottom: the button stopped, each step, the button running. Click a card to set it on the side; + inserts a step. The flow is described in plain words as you change it, and a card with something missing turns red, so a flow cannot be activated half done.

The Approval Designer.

Test on a Record: whether the flow applies, which steps are skipped, who would approve. Nothing is asked.

Get going

How to use it

1Give the Administrator access of Approvals to the people who set up the flows.
2Approvals → Configuration → Approval Flows → New: pick a template and the kind of record, or start from blank.
3Set the cards in the designer, check the flow with Test on a Record, then Activate it.
4From then on, clicking the button asks for the approval; the approvers answer on the record or in Approvals → To Approve.
Good to know

How it works

✓  Code running as the super user is not stopped, as Odoo intends.
✓  Other modules can approve their own documents with the same flows, e.g. the HR requests and loans of the Odoo Mates HR apps.
✓  Works with Odoo 20 Community and Enterprise. On Enterprise, it cannot be installed with the Approvals app of Odoo: use one or the other.
✓  Every feature is covered by automated tests.
Get started

How to install it

1Copy the module to a folder of your own modules, e.g. /opt/odoo/custom-addons, and add that folder to addons_path in odoo.conf.
2Restart Odoo.
3Activate the developer mode, then Apps → Update Apps List.
4Clear the Apps filter, search Approvals and install it.
Do not use Apps → Import Module. It is meant for data modules without Python, and leaves this module half loaded.
Still failing? Write to odoomates@gmail.com with your Odoo version and the error.

Questions we get

How is it different from Automation Studio?
Approvals does one thing: a button waits for the people you choose. Automation Studio draws whole workflows, with waits, emails and activities, and approvals among its steps.
Does it work with my own modules?
Yes. The buttons of any form or list are listed, yours too, with no code to add.
Can the record still change after the approval?
No. It stays locked from the request until the button has run, unless an approval manager unlocks it.
What if an approver is away?
They name a substitute for the dates; reminders and escalation keep the request moving.
Does it work on Enterprise?
Yes, on Community and Enterprise. On Enterprise, use it or the Approvals app of Odoo, not both.
Is it really free?
Yes, all of it, under LGPL-3. There is no paid edition. Reviews, bug reports and ideas are welcome.
