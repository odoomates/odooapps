How it works

Employees on profiles

Add the employees under POS Employees. Their own rules, if any, apply too: the strictest wins.

✓  Employees listed on profiles get their rules.
✓  Their own rules on the employee form apply too.
✓  Employees on no profile and without rules follow their Odoo user.
✓  Checked on the screen and on the server.
In the shop

The cashier at the till decides

Switch cashier: the rules switch with them.

Whoever logs in, the rules of their profiles apply.

Questions we get

Is it really free?
Yes, all of it, under LGPL-3.

What do I need?
The User Access Manager, the Point of Sale and its employee login (pos_hr). Once they are installed, it installs by itself, with the modules it builds on.

Can an employee be on several profiles?
Yes: they combine as the other rules of the User Access Manager.
