Good to know

What to expect before you install.

  • A bridge: it needs Batch Payments (om_account_batch_payment) and Odoo 18 Reconciliation (om_account_reconcile), and installs itself when both are there.
  • A payment without journal entry is cleared with the open items of the invoices it pays, and marked as reconciled when the transaction covers the whole batch.
  • Undoing a reconciliation puts the batch back to sent.
