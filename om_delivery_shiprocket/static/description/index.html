Overview

From the quotation to the doorstep

Rates when you sell, the shipment when you deliver, the tracking until it arrives: all from the standard delivery methods of Odoo.

Live Rates
The rate of the chosen courier on quotations and at the website checkout, from the pickup and delivery pincodes and the weight.
Courier Choice
Recommended by Shiprocket, cheapest, fastest, or always the same courier. Surface or air only, if you wish.
Ship on Validation
Validating the delivery creates the Shiprocket order, assigns the AWB and requests the pickup.
Label and Manifest
The shipping label and the manifest are attached to the delivery as PDF, ready to print.
Cash on Delivery
COD when the order was paid with Cash on Delivery, always, or never; the amount includes the shipping.
Tracking
Tracking link for you and the customer portal, and the latest status in the chatter in one click.
Cancel and Resend
Cancel the shipment in Shiprocket from the delivery, then send it again with a new AWB.
Pickup Locations
Check Connection lists your pickup locations and fills in the name and pincode.
Your Prices
Margins, free shipping above an amount and real-cost invoicing of Odoo apply to the rates.
Features

A closer look

Configuration

Your Shiprocket API user

✓  Email and password of your API user, visible to administrators only.
✓  Pickup location, pickup and manifest options.
✓  Courier choice, COD rule and package type.
Sales

Rate on the quotation

✓  Add shipping, pick the delivery method: the rate comes from Shiprocket.
✓  The same rate at the website checkout.
✓  Converted to your currency when it is not INR.
Inventory

AWB, label and manifest

✓  AWB as tracking reference, courier and Shiprocket order on the delivery.
✓  Label and manifest PDFs in the chatter.
✓  Status button for the latest tracking event.
Setup

Ready in a few minutes

1. Create the delivery method
Inventory, Configuration, Delivery Methods: choose Shiprocket as provider.
2. Enter your account
Your Shiprocket credentials in the Shiprocket Configuration tab, then Check Connection.
3. Set your options
Services, package type, cash on delivery, labels: each option has its help text.
4. Sell and ship
Add shipping on quotations, publish the method for the website, validate deliveries to ship.
