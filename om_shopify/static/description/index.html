In detail

Everything it syncs

Each part can be turned on or off store by store, in the tabs of the store.

Connection
Dev Dashboard app installed by link (OAuth), or a custom app token. Expiring tokens renewed and rotated by Odoo.
Setup Wizard
Credentials, warehouse and payment journal, then the first imports, in three steps.
Product Import
Bulk import of the whole catalogue, changes every day and by webhook, images included.
Product Export
Variants, prices, weight and image; as draft, active or unlisted; published on the Online Store.
CSV / Excel Mapping
Link catalogues whose SKUs and barcodes do not match from a file.
Metafields
Shopify metafields of products, variants, customers and orders mapped to Odoo fields, one or both ways.
Prices
From an Odoo price list, compare-at prices from another, sent every day; fixed prices to Shopify price lists.
Stock Export
Free to use, on hand or forecasted, per location every 15 minutes, with limits per variant.
Stock Import
Compare the stock of Shopify with Odoo, and apply it if you want.
Customers
Imported with their addresses, matched by email, on demand or with the orders.
B2B Companies
Companies and locations of Shopify B2B, with payment terms and tax exemption.
Orders
By webhook, every 15 minutes, or by order number; test and point of sale orders if you want.
Order Lines
Discounts, shipping, taxes, tips, duties, gift cards and custom items, each on its own product.
Order Rules
Workflow and warehouse chosen by payment method, payment or fulfillment status, tag or channel.
Several Payments
An order paid with two methods gets two payments, each in the journal of its method.
Risky Orders
Held as quotations with the reasons of Shopify and an activity for the responsible of the store.
Order Edits
Lines added, removed or changed in Shopify follow until delivery or invoice.
Fulfillment
Validated deliveries fulfill the order in Shopify, with carrier and tracking number, and email the customer.
Cancellations
Cancelled in Shopify, cancelled in Odoo; or cancel in Shopify from Odoo, refund and restock included.
Refunds
Shopify refunds become paid credit notes and returns; refund a credit note in Shopify from Odoo.
Payouts
Bank statement lines, or one entry per payout through a transit account, with fees and adjustments.
Privacy (GDPR)
The privacy webhooks of Shopify answered; redacted customers anonymized if you want.
Digest
The Shopify orders of the period in the KPI digest emails of Odoo.
Security
Secrets stored encrypted, webhooks checked with the signature of Shopify, user and manager rights.

✓  Import orders and products, send the stock in one click.

Orders

Orders that invoice and pay themselves

✓  By webhook in seconds, and every 15 minutes in case one was missed.
✓  A workflow per payment method: confirm, invoice, register the payments.
✓  Order rules by payment or fulfillment status, tag or channel.
✓  Discounts, shipping, tips, duties, gift cards and custom items.
✓  The Shopify order number on the Odoo order, with a prefix of your own.
✓  Orders already fulfilled in Shopify are delivered in Odoo, not fulfilled again.
Taxes and markets

Taxes your way, currencies included

✓  The rates charged by Shopify, or the taxes and fiscal positions of Odoo.
✓  The currency of the store, or the one your customer paid in.
✓  Lines edited in Shopify follow until the order is delivered or invoiced.
✓  New tax rates created on the account of your choice.
Risk

Risky orders wait for you

✓  Kept as a quotation when Shopify recommends to investigate or cancel.
✓  An activity with the reasons for the responsible of the store.
✓  Release it, or cancel it in Shopify, refund included.
Products

Products and variants, both ways

✓  Options become attributes; only the variants that exist in Shopify.
✓  Catalogues with other codes linked from a CSV or Excel file.
✓  Exported with variants, prices, weight and image, published if you want.
✓  Metafields of products and variants sent with them.
Settings

Matching and prices, store by store

✓  Match on SKU, then barcode; create the missing products with images.
✓  Prices from a price list, compare-at prices from another, sent every day.
✓  Exported as draft, active or unlisted, on the Online Store if you want.
Stock

The right quantity in every location

✓  Free to use, on hand or forecasted, only for the products that moved.
✓  Per variant: at most a quantity, a percentage or a quantity kept back.
✓  Or count the stock of Shopify in Odoo.
✓  Each Shopify location sends the stock of its own warehouse.
✓  Orders shipped from the warehouse of their Shopify location.
Payouts

Payouts in the bank journal

✓  Each charge and refund with its customer and invoice, fees on one line.
✓  Or one journal entry per payout through a transit account.
✓  Ready for the bank reconciliation of Odoo.
✓  Fee, adjustment and clearing accounts of your choice.
Jobs

Nothing gets lost

✓  Every import, export and webhook is a job; a failed job leaves nothing half done.

✓  Failed jobs show their error, run again in one click, and give an activity.
How it works

Connected in three steps

1
Create a Shopify app
In the Shopify Dev Dashboard, with the scopes listed in the questions below, and install it on your store.
2
Connect the store
Shopify → Configuration → Connect a Store: address, client ID and secret, warehouse and journal.
3
Import and sync
Import the products and the orders, send the stock, and sync the webhooks.
Install

Get started

How to install it

1Copy the module to a folder of your own modules, e.g. /opt/odoo/custom-addons, and add that folder to addons_path in odoo.conf.
2Restart Odoo.
3Activate the developer mode, then Apps → Update Apps List.
4Clear the Apps filter, search om_shopify and install it.
It installs Connector Base (om_connector_base), Sales and Inventory with it.
Do not use Apps → Import Module. It is meant for data modules without Python, and leaves this module half loaded.

Questions we get

Which Shopify app do I need?
An app made in the Shopify Dev Dashboard (client ID and secret), or a custom app made in the admin of your store (access token). Scopes: read/write_products, read/write_inventory, read_locations, read/write_orders, read_customers, read/write_fulfillments and the merchant managed fulfillment orders; write_publications to publish, read_shopify_payments_payouts for the payouts.
Do I need webhooks?
No, but they make orders arrive in seconds. Shopify sends them to an https address only: set the public address of your Odoo on the store and click Sync Webhooks. Without them, orders come every 15 minutes.
Can I connect several stores?
Yes, as many as you want, in one or several companies, each with its own warehouse, workflows, price lists and settings.
What if an order is missed?
Orders are also fetched every 15 minutes from the last import, and any order can be imported by its number. A webhook sent twice runs once.
How are refunds handled?
A refund in Shopify becomes a credit note, paid in the journal of the payment method, with a return of the restocked products. A credit note refunded from Odoo is refunded in Shopify, with or without restock.
Who can use it?
Two levels of rights: Shopify users see the stores, orders and jobs; managers set up the stores and see the credentials, which are stored encrypted.
Which API does it use?
The Shopify Admin GraphQL API, with a version you choose on the store. Odoo waits when Shopify limits the calls, instead of failing.
Do I need Odoo Enterprise?
No. It runs on the Community Edition, and installs on Enterprise as well.
