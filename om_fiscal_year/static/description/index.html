Fiscal years

A year that matches your books, not the calendar

Set the start and end date of the financial year and generate its periods. Every report that offers this financial year then follows it.

  • ✓  Any start month: January, April, July, whatever your country or group uses.
  • ✓  Monthly or quarterly periods, generated in one action.
  • ✓  Several years open at once while the previous one is being audited.

A fiscal year and the periods generated inside it.

Closing

Close a period once it is reconciled and reported

Period closing marks a period as done, and the year-end closing carries the result of the year to the retained earnings account and opens the next one.

  • ✓  A closing task list of what is left to do.
  • ✓  The year-end entry generated for you.
  • ✓  Reopen a period if something has to be corrected, with the trail kept.

Period closing.

Lock dates

The cheapest control in accounting

Three lock dates decide who can still post before a date.

  • ✓  Journal Entries Lock Date: nobody but an accounting manager.
  • ✓  All Users Lock Date: nobody at all, managers included.
  • ✓  Tax Return Lock Date: nothing that would change a filed return.
  • ✓  Move them forward each month as soon as the period is reported.
