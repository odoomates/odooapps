=========================================================
AI CV Reading - Fill Applicants from CVs with Your Own AI
=========================================================

Reads CVs with your own AI provider or a local model of the AI engine.

* **Read CV with AI** on an applicant with the CV attached (PDF, image or
  text), or **Read CVs with AI** in the Actions menu for several applicants.
* Empty fields are filled: name, email, phone, LinkedIn profile, degree
  (among your degrees), availability, and tags (only existing tags). Values
  already set are never replaced; another email in the CV is flagged.
* A summary, the experience, the education, the skills and the languages are
  added to the notes of the applicant.
* What to check is shown on the applicant.
* Usage is logged under the feature ``extract``.
