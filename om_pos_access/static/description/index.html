POS Access Control

Decide what each cashier may do in the Point of Sale: refunds, discounts, prices, pricelists, cancelling, cash, closing, customers, payment methods.
Set on the user. The buttons leave the screen, and the server refuses what is sent anyway.

A cashier without discounts or price changes: the % and Price keys are off.

What it controls

What a cashier may not do

Tick what applies to each user, in a Point of Sale tab of the user form.

↩

Refunds

The Refund button goes, or refunds only from the receipt of the sale: the +/- key cannot give money back. Refunds sent anyway are refused.
%

Discounts

No discount at all, or a maximum per line: a higher one is brought down to it.
🏷

Prices

No price change, or a maximum cut below the pricelist, checked before paying.
📈

Never below cost

A price or a discount that sells for less than the cost is refused.
💵

Cash in and out

Cash In/Out leaves the menu and the closing popup; cash moves sent anyway are refused.
🔒

Closing the register

Close Register leaves the menu; closing sent anyway is refused.
👤

Customers

No creating or editing customers from the shop. Choosing one stays possible.
➖

Lowering quantities

Once a product is on the order, it cannot be removed or lowered: a manager has to.
💳

Payment methods

Only the methods allowed are shown, and orders paid otherwise are refused.
📋

Pricelists

Only the pricelists allowed can be chosen, and orders with another one are refused: the price limits cannot be dodged.
🗑

Cancelling orders

Cancel Order goes, and an order with products cannot be deleted from the list of orders.
💰

Refund limit

Refunds up to an amount per order, taxes included; a bigger one is refused.
Set on the user

One tab on the user form

Open the user, Point of Sale tab, and tick. The next time they open the shop, it applies.

Enforced on the server

Not just hidden

A button off the screen is not enough: the server checks what the shop sends.

✓  The buttons leave the screen: cashiers do not see what they may not use.
✓  The server checks every order, cash move and closing a cashier sends, and refuses what is not allowed.
✓  Refunding a discounted sale stays possible, as do combo parts, rewards and tips.
✓  Users and employees without rules keep the shop as it is: managers are not slowed down.

Questions we get

Is it really free?
Yes, all of it, under LGPL-3.

Do I need the User Access Manager?
No. POS Access Control works alone, with the Point of Sale only. With the User Access Manager, add User Access Manager for POS to set the rules on its profiles.

Our cashiers log in with a badge or a PIN.
Add POS Access Control for Employees: each employee gets the same rules, or follows their Odoo user.

What about managers?
A user with no rule keeps the shop as it is.

Can a cashier get around it?
Orders, cash moves and closings sent to the server are checked there too, and refused when not allowed.

Which versions?
Odoo 18, Community and Enterprise.
