Leases: Right-of-use Assets

Links Loans and Leases with Assets Management: the right-of-use asset of each lease is created when the lease is recognized, at the lease liability, and depreciated over the lease term.

Installs by itself Asset at the lease liability Depreciated over the lease term
Loans and Leases Install
What it adds

The right of use, created and depreciated for you

✓ A Right-of-use Asset Category on the lease (Accounting tab): the recognition debits the asset account of the category, Dr Right-of-use asset / Cr Long-term lease liability.
✓ The asset is created at the same time: its value is the lease liability (in the company currency), from the start of the lease.
✓ Depreciated linearly, prorata temporis, to the end of the lease term, with the journal, the accounts and the period of the category.
✓ Confirmed when the category confirms its assets, else left in draft to check.
✓ The Right of Use button of the lease opens it; cancelling the recognition cancels it.
Example
Lease 12 monthly payments of 1,000.00 at 6% from 1 January 2025
Lease liability 11,618.93
Asset 11,618.93, depreciated 968.24 at the end of each month of 2025

Without a category, the lease debits its Right-of-use Asset Account and no asset is created.

The lease: the Right of Use button at the top, the asset category in the Accounting tab.

The right-of-use asset of a 36-month lease: one depreciation a month to the end of the lease term.

The asset: linear, prorata temporis, ending with the lease.

Setup

One asset category

Create an asset category for the rights of use (Invoicing › Configuration › Asset Categories): the right-of-use asset account, its accumulated depreciation and depreciation expense accounts, the journal and the period of the depreciations. Then choose it on the lease before confirming it.

Install

Nothing to do, most of the time

✓ It needs Loans and Leases and Odoo 20 Assets Management.
✓ It installs by itself when both apps are installed. If they already are, install it from the Apps menu (developer mode, Update Apps List, search om_account_loan_asset).
✓ The menus are under Invoicing › Accounting, shown to the users with Show Full Accounting Features.
