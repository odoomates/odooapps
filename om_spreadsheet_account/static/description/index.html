Accounting Dashboards for Odoo 20 Community

Five spreadsheet dashboards in the Finance section of the Dashboards app — the overview of the year, the profit and loss, the balance sheet, the receivables and payables, and the bank and cash — all computed from the account types, so they work with any chart of accounts.

Overview

The figures of the year, on opening the app

Revenue, gross profit, expenses and net profit, next to the bank balance, the receivables and the payables, over the period you choose and compared with the same period last year or with the one before it.

  • ✓  Any period: a year, a quarter, a month, or dates of your own.
  • ✓  The companies picked in the switcher are added up.
  • ✓  Read by the accounting users and the accounting managers.
  • ✓  Sample figures are shown until there is anything in the books.

The Accounting Overview dashboard.

Dashboards

Four more, on the statements that matter

  • ✓  Profit and Loss: income, expenses and net profit by month, with the gross and net margins.
  • ✓  Balance Sheet: assets, liabilities and equity against the comparison date, with the current ratio, the quick ratio and the debt to equity.
  • ✓  Receivables and Payables: the open balances by age, the days sales and days payables outstanding, and the largest overdue customers and vendors.
  • ✓  Bank and Cash: the balances, the cash in and out by month, and what is left to reconcile on each journal.

Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, Receivables and Payables, Bank and Cash.

Your own figures

A formula for the spreadsheets you build yourself

OM.ACCOUNT.BALANCE gives the balance of the accounts of the types you name, over a period, for the companies you have picked — the same figure the dashboards are built on, in any cell of any spreadsheet.

  • ✓  One or several account types, from the first day to the last one you give.
  • ✓  Posted entries only, or the unposted ones as well.
  • ✓  The result is formatted in the currency of the company.
  • ✓  Add the Assets, Budgets and Follow-ups dashboards by installing the modules that go with them.

The Assets, Budgets, and Follow-ups and Cash Forecast dashboards, added by the modules that go with them.
