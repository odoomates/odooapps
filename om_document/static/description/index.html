How it works

Type, file, be reminded

No more expired licenses found too late.

1

Create the types

Trade License, Passport, Insurance Certificate: the kind of record they belong to, their fields and the reminder days.
2

Keep the documents

The number, the issue and expiry dates and the files of each document, on its record.
3

Be reminded

To-dos before the expiry, to the people the record names; renew the document when the new one arrives.

At a glance

What needs renewing

Expiring Documents, Documents: Expiring Documents and Expired Documents show what needs renewing; All Documents lists every document. A document is expiring once the first reminder of its type is due.

The document

Its number, its dates, its files

Open a document to add its files, and use Renew when the new one arrives: the new document keeps a link to the one it replaces, which is archived.

Document types

Reminded on the days you choose

Each type says what it has (a number, an issue date, an expiry date) and when to remind, e.g. 30, 7 and 0 days before. The reminders go as to-dos to the people the record names, e.g. the HR responsible of an employee, else to the responsible users of the type; optionally, an email to the holder of the document as well.

Features

Everything an expiring document needs

From the first scan to the renewal.

📁

Types per kind of record

With or without a number, an issue date and an expiry date.
⏰

Reminders before the expiry

On the days you choose per type; each sent once, and again after the expiry date changes. A daily scheduled action sends them.
📧

The holder told too

Optionally, an email to the holder of the document.
🖼️

Valid, expiring, expired

A kanban with a preview of the files, coloured badges, and a sidebar by type.
🔄

Renewal history

The renewal links to the document it replaces, which is archived.
📎

A Documents widget

On the records keeping them: a card per document and per missing type; drop a file on a card to attach it.
🏢

Multi-company

A document belongs to the company of its record; a type without a company is shared by all.
🔧

For developers

Any model keeps documents by inheriting document.mixin, as HR Documents does for the employees.

With the other Odoo Mates apps

Prints filed as documents

With Print Templates, the bridge Print Templates: Documents installs by itself: a template files each print as a document of the record, e.g. a No Objection Certificate valid for 30 days. Document Expiry works without it.

How to use it

1.  Give the Administrator access of Document Expiry to the people who manage all the documents.
2.  Expiring Documents, Configuration, Document Types: the kind of record (Applies To), the fields they have, the reminder days (e.g. 30,7,0), Notify the Holder, and the Responsible Users reminded when the record names no one.
3.  Expiring Documents, Documents: Expiring Documents and Expired Documents show what needs renewing.
4.  Open a document to add its files, and use Renew when the new one arrives.

Good to know

✓  Depends only on Discuss (mail): it installs by itself, without any other Odoo Mates app.
✓  The Documents widget shows on the records of the models that inherit document.mixin; another module adds it, and names who is reminded (_document_responsible_users) and who holds the documents (_document_holder_partner).
✓  Works with Odoo 20 Community and Enterprise.
