Forecast

What the bank account will hold, period by period

One column per period, from the opening balance of the bank and cash accounts to the closing balance: the receipts, the payments and the net cash flow of each period, and the total of the whole forecast.

  • ✓  Receipts from the customer invoices, the recurring receipts and the other items you expect.
  • ✓  Payments from the vendor bills, the recurring payments and the other items you expect.
  • ✓  By weeks or by months, from any starting date, over up to 60 periods.
  • ✓  The invoices and bills already due get their own Overdue column, or are left out of the forecast.
  • ✓  Count the draft invoices and bills on their due date as well, when you want the worst case.
  • ✓  Detail each row by partner, to see who owes what and when.
  • ✓  Printed as a PDF, and exported to Excel with the Accounting Excel Reports module.

Expected items

The money that is not in an invoice yet

Salaries, rent, loan instalments, taxes, a subsidy on its way — everything you know is coming but that no document holds yet, entered once and counted in every forecast.

  • ✓  A receipt or a payment, with its amount, its currency, its date and the partner it concerns.
  • ✓  Once, or every week, month, quarter or year, until a date or without end.
  • ✓  A monthly item dated on the 31st stays at the end of each month, whatever its length.
  • ✓  Amounts in a foreign currency are converted at the rate of the day they are expected on.
  • ✓  Archive an item that no longer applies instead of deleting it.

The expected items: what is coming in and going out, once or every month.
