Your AI, your key
Built on the free AI Engine (om_ai): the requests go from your Odoo server straight to the provider you choose, with your key, or to a model server on your own machine. Nothing goes through Odoo Mates. Every call is in the AI usage log, within your daily limits.

Bank statements from PDFs and photos

Upload a statement: the transactions are listed with their dates, labels, partners and amounts, with the balances. Balances that do not add up, another currency or account, or a low confidence are shown. Edit, then create the statement, ready to reconcile, with the file attached.
How

Read, check, review

The same steps for every document.

Text or vision
PDFs are read from their text, photos and scans by a vision model.
Checked in code
A JSON schema per document; types, dates, numbers and allowed values are checked.
Confidence and flags
The AI says how sure it is and what is unclear; totals are checked.
Review first
Nothing is posted or submitted automatically.
Bridges

Bills, receipts and CVs

Installed automatically with the matching app.

Vendor bills

With Vendor Bill OCR: choose the AI engine in the settings and the uploaded bills are read by your model, the vendor, the taxes and the accounts found as before.

Expense receipts

With Expenses: the merchant, the date, the total, the currency and the category of a receipt photo, for the employee to check.

CVs

With Recruitment: the contact details, the degree, the tags and a summary of the experience, filled on the applicant.
Good to know
  • You need the AI Engine (om_ai) with chat and vision models that support JSON output.
  • At most 5 pages of a PDF are read, 3 pages of a scan; scanned PDFs need poppler-utils on the server.
  • Usage is logged under the feature "extract". Check the readings with your own provider and model.
