==================
Quick Add Products
==================

Quick ways to add products to a document with lines, next to the Catalog button of its lines:

* **Paste lines**: rows copied from Excel, an email or a message, one product per row with its barcode,
  reference or name, the quantity and, if wanted, the price. The rows are checked first: found, to choose
  between several products, or not found.
* **Scan to add**: a barcode scanned outside a text field adds the product, or raises its quantity by one.
* **History**: the products the customer bought (or the vendor supplied) with their last date, quantity and
  price; type the quantities to add, fill the last quantities, or repeat the last document.
* **Copy lines**: the lines of another document, all or some, at today's prices or the prices copied.

A product already on the document, in the same unit and at the same price, gets its quantity raised instead
of a second line. A price pasted or kept stays on the line like a price typed by hand.

The documents come with their own modules, installed by themselves with their app: Quick Add Products - Sales
(sales orders), - Purchase (purchase orders), - Invoicing (invoices and bills) and - Inventory (transfers).
