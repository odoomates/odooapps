Rental - Planning

Rent identified units, such as machines, vehicles or rooms, with their availability from the planning: a unit in maintenance, on a job or on another rental is never offered twice.

Units, not just quantities

Choose per product: by quantity like before, or by units booked in the planning.

By Units
Equipment, vehicles or any resource are the units of the product.
Booked on Confirm
Free units booked from pickup to return, preparation included.
Next Free Date

Late Returns
The booking runs on and clashes with the next rental.
Delivery Crew
Delivery and collection slots for a role of the product.
Maintenance Aware
Scheduled maintenance blocks the unit.
Change the Unit
On the order line, or by moving the booking on the Gantt.
Unit Schedule
Rentals, maintenance and other bookings per unit.
