Print

The cheques of your bank, not of a standard form

Print the cheques from the vendor payments the way Odoo does it, with the layout taken from the cheque format of the bank journal. Nothing but the fields is printed: the paper already carries the rest.

  • ✓  Date, payee, amount in figures, amount in words, memo, cheque number, company name and any fixed text you need.
  • ✓  Each field placed in millimetres, with its own width, height, font size, bold, alignment, letter spacing, rotation and, if you want, a border.
  • ✓  Letter spacing puts the digits of a date in the boxes printed on the cheque.
  • ✓  Payment stubs on the rest of the page, listing the bills the cheque pays, one stub or two.
  • ✓  A journal without a format of its own takes the first format of the country of its company.

Formats

Ready for the cheques of most countries

The module ships with the usual layouts — cheque on top, in the middle or at the bottom of an A4 or a Letter page, and the cheque leaves of the US, Canada, the UK and Ireland, the Gulf, Australia and New Zealand. Duplicate the closest one and move the fields to match your own cheque.

  • ✓  A4, US Letter, or the exact size of a cheque leaf in millimetres.
  • ✓  Eleven date formats, including the ones written digit by digit in boxes.
  • ✓  Stars around the amount and along the words line, so nothing can be added afterwards.
  • ✓  Cents in words or as a fraction, capitals, a suffix such as Only, and a second line of words for a bilingual cheque.
  • ✓  The words in a language of their own — English on the cheques of the Gulf whatever the language of the user, Arabic on the second line.
  • ✓  A format belongs to one company, or is shared by all of them, and can be tied to the countries it is used in.

Calibrate

Line it up before you waste a cheque

The format shows a preview with sample values, laid out on the page as it will be printed, and it follows every change you make. Two millimetre offsets move everything right and down to make up for what your printer does.

  • ✓  Print the test page on plain paper, hold it against a real cheque, and adjust.
  • ✓  The paper format of the report is kept in step with the size of the page, without margins, so the positions are measured from the edges.
  • ✓  For blank cheque paper, the MICR line can be printed at the bottom in E-13B characters, US (ANSI X9) or Canadian (CPA-005), from an E-13B font of your own.
  • ✓  On the pre-printed cheques of your bank, leave the MICR line off: it is already there.
