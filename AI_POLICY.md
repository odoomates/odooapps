# AI Contribution Policy

## In short

- You may use AI tools (chat assistants, code completion, agents) to help you.
- **You are responsible for every line you submit**, however it was produced.
- If you cannot explain and defend a change, do not submit it.
- No unsupervised bots: no automated mass issues, pull requests or comments.
- AI tools are never authors or co-authors of a commit.

## The rules

**A person stands behind every contribution.** Issues, pull requests, reviews
and comments come from a person who has read them, understood them and checked
that they are correct. "The AI wrote it" is not an answer to a review comment.

**Test what you submit.** Generated code often looks right and is not. Run the
tests of the module, install it on a fresh database and try the change in the
interface before you open the pull request.

**Keep it small and real.** A contribution fixes a real problem or adds a real
improvement, with a tight scope. Large generated changes, rewrites of working
code, or reformatting of whole files are closed without review.

**Answer the review yourself.** When a maintainer asks a question, answer it.
Regenerating the whole change and pushing it again is not an answer.

**Check generated text.** Bug reports, descriptions and comments that were
written with an AI tool must be checked by you: wrong versions, invented error
messages and steps that do not reproduce waste everyone's time.

**Authorship.** Do not add AI tools in `Co-authored-by:` or any other author
field of a commit. You may mention in the pull request description that an AI
tool helped, if you wish.

## Consequences

Contributions that do not follow this policy are closed. Accounts that behave
like bots (mass issues, pull requests, reviews or comments) are blocked.

See also the [Contributing guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the
[Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
