Odoo 20 Accounting Audit

Data inalterability checks, entries secured on closing, and an audit pack.

  • The data inalterability report and the securing of the entries from the Review menu.
  • A weekly integrity check, kept in a history; changed entries are reported to the administrators.
  • The period closing checks the entries not secured, and is blocked by changed entries.
  • The entries of a period secured when it is closed, on request.
  • An Audit Pack in PDF: integrity, entries, lock dates, closings, changes and accesses.
