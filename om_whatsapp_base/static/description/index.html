WhatsApp for Odoo 18

The base module of the Odoo Mates WhatsApp Connector: WhatsApp templates, a composer on every document, and a log of every message sent and received.

This module alone: Click to Chat. Click Send and WhatsApp opens in your browser (or the app on a phone) with the message filled in; you press Send there, and it is logged in Odoo.

Available on this base: WhatsApp Connector Web (om_whatsapp_web) and WhatsApp Connector QR (om_whatsapp_device), for files, replies in Odoo and automation. Coming soon: WhatsApp Connector Meta (om_whatsapp).

This module holds the templates, the composer and the messages. How a message reaches WhatsApp is set on each WhatsApp account:

Included

Click to Chat

Opens the chat with the text filled in; you press Send in WhatsApp. Nothing else to install.

Available

WhatsApp Web

With WhatsApp Connector Web (om_whatsapp_web) and the Odoo Mates WhatsApp Connector extension: texts and PDFs sent from WhatsApp Web open in your browser, without leaving Odoo. No Meta API, no sign-up.

Coming soon

Meta Cloud API

With WhatsApp Connector Meta (om_whatsapp): the official Cloud API of Meta, from the server, for automation rules and any number of records, with templates approved by Meta. Same templates, same conversations.

Available

WhatsApp QR

With WhatsApp Connector QR (om_whatsapp_device): scan a QR code, and Odoo sends and receives from your server, as a linked device of your WhatsApp, with no browser open. On premise.

Why this one

More than a click-to-chat link

Many WhatsApp apps open a chat with the text filled in, and that is all: nothing is kept in Odoo. This one keeps WhatsApp in Odoo.

  • ✓  Every message is logged on its document, with its status, and listed in WhatsApp > Messages.
  • ✓  One conversation a customer in Discuss, with the messages sent from documents and the replies.
  • ✓  Templates for any document with a chatter, filled with its values, and its PDF report.
  • ✓  Needs only Discuss: no Sales, Invoicing or Website app to install.
  • ✓  Customers who opted out are skipped, and every account has its own users.
  • ✓  Community and Enterprise: installs side by side with the WhatsApp app of Odoo.
Composer

Send from a contact, or from any document

Action > Send WhatsApp on a contact, or on any document a template is made for. The number is found on the document or on its customer.

  • ✓  Pick a template: the text is filled with the values of the document, and stays editable.
  • ✓  Attach files, or the PDF of the report of the template: with WhatsApp Connector Web, Meta or QR.
  • ✓  Numbers of the phone blacklist are skipped: customers who opted out are not contacted.

The composer on a quotation: the template fills the text with its values.

Templates

Write it once, send it everywhere

A template is the text sent for one kind of document, with its values: Hello {{ object.name }}.

  • ✓  For contacts, sales orders, invoices, or any document with a chatter.
  • ✓  Add Action Menu puts the template in the Action menu of its documents.
  • ✓  Translated in the language of the customer, and able to attach a printed report.

A template for quotations, which attaches the quotation PDF.

Every message logged

Know what was sent, to whom, and how it went

WhatsApp > Messages lists every message, sent and received, with its status: queued, sent, delivered, read, or failed with the reason. Click to Chat records it as sent once the chat opens; delivered, read and the replies come with WhatsApp Connector Web, Meta or QR.

  • ✓  A sent message is logged on the chatter of its document, with its ticks; a reply goes to the chatter of the document it answers, and to the WhatsApp conversation of the number in Discuss.
  • ✓  A failed message is sent again from its status, with one click.
  • ✓  Accounts list the users who send from them and share their conversations; a user sees the messages of the documents they can read.

Every WhatsApp message, with its status.

The messages on the chatter of the contact, with their ticks, and its WhatsApp button, which opens the conversation.

Conversations

Every customer, one chat in Discuss

Discuss gets a WhatsApp tab: one conversation a number, shared by the users of the account, with what was sent from the documents and what the customer answered.

  • ✓  Answer from Discuss: what you type goes out on WhatsApp.
  • ✓  Quoted replies, reactions and read ticks, as on the phone.
  • ✓  An unknown number gets a conversation too, with a button to make its contact.

A conversation in Discuss. Replies come in with WhatsApp Web, the Meta Cloud API or WhatsApp QR; Click to Chat logs what you send.

Questions we get

Is this module enough to send?
Yes, with Click to Chat: the chat opens with the text filled in and you press Send. To send texts and PDFs without leaving Odoo, and get the replies, use WhatsApp Connector Web (om_whatsapp_web) and its free browser extension.

Can a WhatsApp go by itself, when an order is confirmed?
Yes, with an automation rule, and WhatsApp Connector QR, which sends it by itself (WhatsApp Connector Meta, coming soon, will too); WhatsApp Connector Web sends it when a user triggers the rule (confirms the order), one message at a time. Click to Chat alone cannot, as someone has to press Send. To set it up, install the Automation Rules app of Odoo (the WhatsApp modules do not install it), then, in developer mode, go to Settings > Technical > Automation Rules and create a rule on Sales Order, triggered when the State is set to Sales Order, with an action of type Send WhatsApp and your template; its report goes as a PDF. The same works on invoices, deliveries and any document with a chatter.

Does it need Meta, or a WhatsApp Business API account?
No. Click to Chat needs none, nor do WhatsApp Connector Web and WhatsApp Connector QR. For those who want the official Meta Cloud API, sending from the server, WhatsApp Connector Meta (om_whatsapp) is coming soon, as a separate module.

Does it work on Odoo Enterprise?
Yes, on Community and Enterprise. If you do not want the WhatsApp app of Odoo, which needs a Meta business account, use this one: its models have their own names (om.whatsapp.*), so both can even be installed side by side.
