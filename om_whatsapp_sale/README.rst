==============================
WhatsApp Quotations and Orders
==============================

Quotations and order confirmations by WhatsApp, through the Odoo Mates
WhatsApp connector (any provider it supports). Installed by itself when Sales
and the WhatsApp connector are both installed.

* **Send by WhatsApp** on a quotation: the PDF and the link to view, accept,
  sign and pay it online. The quotation is marked as sent, as by email, when
  the message goes.
* **Send by WhatsApp** on a sales order: the order confirmation.
* **Automatic confirmation** (optional, *Sales > Configuration > Settings >
  WhatsApp*): each order goes to the customer once, when it is confirmed, by a
  salesperson, an online signature or an online payment.

The messages are WhatsApp templates you can edit or replace. A customer
without a valid mobile number gets nothing automatically; opted-out numbers are
skipped; a problem when sending never stops the confirmation. An order built by
the WhatsApp AI assistant is confirmed in its own conversation and is not sent
twice.
