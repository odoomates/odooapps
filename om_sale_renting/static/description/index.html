Rental

Rent out equipment, vehicles, tents or rooms from Sales: prices per hour, day, week or month, pickup and return, late fees and a schedule of what is out.

Everything a rental business needs

A rental order is a sales order with a pickup and a return date: quotations, invoicing and the portal work as usual.

Rental Prices
Per hour, day, week, month or year, per variant or pricelist.
Best Price
The cheapest mix for the period: 9 days = 1 week + 2 days.
Pickup and Return
Wizards with quantities, notes and serial numbers.
Late Fees
Extra hour and extra day prices, added at return.
Availability
Warning when the quantity is not free, with the next free date.
Schedule
Calendar of the rentals by product.
Analysis
Revenue and utilisation per day, product and customer.
Agreement and Emails
Rental agreement printout, confirmation and reminders.

See it at work

Screens from a test database with the demo data.

Rental orders by status
Quotations, reserved, picked up and returned, with the late ones flagged.
✓  Pickup and return dates on every order.
✓  Filters for today's pickups and returns.
✓  List, kanban, calendar and pivot views.
Price for the period
Each line gets the cheapest combination of the rental prices that covers the period.
✓  The period and the charged duration are written on the line.
✓  Change the dates: prices and descriptions follow.
✓  The free quantity is shown on each line.
Availability warning
Other confirmed rentals of the period are deducted, preparation time included.
✓  Late rentals stay busy until they come back.
✓  The next date the quantity is free is proposed.
✓  With Inventory, the stock of the warehouse is used.
Rental products
Tick Can be Rented on any good or service and set its prices.
✓  Prices per variant or pricelist.
✓  Extra hour and day prices for late returns.
✓  Minimum duration and preparation time.
Pickup
Choose the quantities, and with Inventory the serial numbers, then validate.
✓  Stock moves to the rental location of the warehouse.
✓  Serials reserved by other rentals are not proposed.
✓  Partial pickups are possible.
Return with late fees
The delay and the late fee of each product are computed from its extra prices.
✓  Late fees added to the order in one click.
✓  Damages or extra charges with a description.
✓  The same serials are expected back.
Rental schedule
What is out and when, by product.
✓  Week, month or year.
✓  Filter by product.
✓  Opens the rental line.
Utilisation and revenue
Units rented and revenue spread over the days of each rental.
✓  Pivot and graph views.
✓  By product, category, customer, salesperson.
✓  Utilisation as a share of the rentable quantity.
Rental agreement
A printout with the pickup and return dates, the products, your terms and signatures.
✓  Sent with the confirmation email.
✓  Terms and conditions set in the settings.
✓  The rental period also shows on the quotation.
Customer portal
Customers see the period and the status of their rentals.
✓  Rental period in My Orders.
✓  Pickup and return reminders by email.
✓  Online signature and payment as for sales orders.
