The heart of it

Configure everything from the menu tree

The Menus & Apps tab shows the real menus of your database. Untick a menu to hide it with everything below. Click a menu and decide, beside the tree, what its users may do there. Saved at once.

✓  Read, create, edit, delete, and which records: their own, their team, their companies, drafts only...
✓  Each field visible, read only, hidden or required, always or only when a condition holds.
✓  Each button visible, hidden or blocked on the server; tabs, filters, views, reports, Action menu.
✓  One level for a whole app: full access, no configuration, read only, no access.

Quotations: their own records, no delete; the marks show a field hidden and Cancel blocked.

An app their user form leaves out shows Not given: tick it to give it, and restrict it like any other.

New: Health Check

Know your rules still hold, every week

Apps come and go, Odoo moves on. The Health Check runs on its own every week, or in one click from the menu, a profile or the list, and tells you what needs a look. It never changes a profile by itself: you decide.

✓  Rules lost with the menu, data or field they named: restore them in one click.
✓  Rules matching nothing: a tab, filter or button no view has any more.
✓  Broken filters naming a field that is gone, before your users run into them.
✓  All good? It says so, and the profiles stay as they are.

What it controls

From a whole app down to one button

Data and records

Read, create, edit, delete. Their own, their team in the org chart, their sales team, their companies, their warehouse, this month, drafts only, or any filter.

Allowed records

Only these journals, shops, warehouses, locations, sales teams, and what belongs to them. Per profile, or each user their own.

Fields

Hidden, read only or required, always or under a condition. One switch for every cost and margin, one for the sales prices.

Buttons, reports, actions

Hide or block any button, kanban links included, any report, any Action menu entry, or the whole Print or Action menu.

Chatter and activities

Hide the chatter, Send message, Log note, followers, attachments, activities: everywhere or for one data, refused on the server.

The Settings

Hide one app of the Settings (Sales, Inventory...) from a Settings user, and what only it holds cannot be saved.

The screen

Tabs, filters, view types, status bars; no export, import, archiving, duplication, custom filters or saved searches.

Who gets a profile

Users, an Odoo group, new users, users for a while (a replacement during a leave), and only in some companies.

Several profiles

They combine like Odoo groups; an override profile, such as Read Only, always wins.

Upgrading Odoo?

Keep it installed. Your profiles come along.

Profiles, users, versions and sign-in rules are ordinary records: they move with your database. A menu, a field or a button a new Odoo no longer has can take a rule with it; the Health Check finds those for you.

1  Keep the module installed.
2  Upgrade a copy of the database first.
3  Run the Health Check, set again what it lists.
4  Check with Preview as User, and go live.

Questions we get

Is it really free?
Yes, all of it, under LGPL-3. There is no paid edition.

Is a hidden field really hidden?
Yes. It leaves the screens, and the server blanks it in everything a user, the browser console or a script reads, and refuses to filter, group or export on it.

Can a user carry several profiles?
Yes. They combine like Odoo groups; an override profile always applies. Preview as User shows the result.

Which templates will I get?
Those of the apps you installed: Sales, CRM, Invoicing, Inventory, Purchase, Point of Sale, Manufacturing, Project, Timesheets, Expenses, Employees, plus an auditor and, for any app, a user without configuration or a read only one. Install an app later, and its template appears.

Who manages the profiles?
The members of the Access Management group, in the Access Manager app: the Administrator from the start, and anyone you add. No Settings rights needed.

Can I move my profiles to production?
Yes: export them from a test database and import them on production. What production lacks is listed on the profile.

Will it survive an Odoo upgrade?
Keep it installed: the profiles move with the database. Run the Health Check on the upgraded copy and set again what it lists.

Can Quotations and Orders have different rights?
Not on the server: both open the same data, and Odoo checks rights per data. Hide one menu, or set a condition on a field or a button.

Does it install anything else?
Only our free User Audit & Sessions app, which logs every refusal of a profile with the user, the record and the address. Everything else comes with Odoo itself.

What happens when I uninstall it?
Every restriction ends, and every group a profile granted is taken back. The groups of the user forms are never touched.
