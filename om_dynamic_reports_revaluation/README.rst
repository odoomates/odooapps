=================================================
Dynamic Accounting Reports - Currency Revaluation
=================================================

Adds the **Unrealized Currency Gains/Losses** report to the Dynamic Accounting
Reports, from Currency Revaluation.

* What is open in a foreign currency at a date, per currency, account and
  partner: the amount in the currency, in the books, at the closing rate of the
  date, and the difference, the unrealized gain (positive) or loss (negative).
* The open items of the receivable and payable accounts count for what is left
  on them at the date, the other accounts for their balance; the figures are
  those of the currency revaluation.
* **Post the adjustment** (the "..." menu of a currency) opens the currency
  revaluation of the date for that currency, to check and post its entry.
* Click a gain or a loss to see the journal items behind it.

The module installs by itself with Dynamic Accounting Reports and Currency
Revaluation.
