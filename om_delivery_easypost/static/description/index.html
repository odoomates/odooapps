Overview

From the quotation to the doorstep

Rates when you sell, the shipment when you deliver, the tracking until it arrives: all from the standard delivery methods of Odoo.

Many Carriers, One Account
All the carrier accounts of your EasyPost account, or only the ones you list.
Live Rates
On quotations and at the website checkout: the cheapest or the fastest rate of the carriers and services you allow.
Labels
Bought on validation, attached to the delivery in PDF, ZPL for thermal printers, or PNG, in the size you choose.
Customs
Customs items for international shipments: HS code and country of origin from the product, contents type, signer.
Insurance
Insure each shipment for a percentage of its value with the Insurance field of the delivery method.
Multi-Package
One label and one tracking number per package when the delivery is packed in several packages.
Test and Production
Your test key in the test environment, your production key in production: one click to switch.
Tracking
The EasyPost tracking page for you and the customer, and the latest status in the chatter.
Refunds
Cancelling the shipment asks EasyPost for the refund of the label.

Setup

Ready in a few minutes

1. Create the delivery method
Inventory, Configuration, Delivery Methods: choose EasyPost as provider.
2. Enter your account
Your EasyPost credentials in the EasyPost Configuration tab, then Check Connection.
3. Set your options
Services, package type, cash on delivery, labels: each option has its help text.
4. Sell and ship
Add shipping on quotations, publish the method for the website, validate deliveries to ship.
