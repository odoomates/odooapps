Website Subscriptions

Sell subscriptions on your online shop: customers pick a recurring plan, pay once at checkout with a saved payment method, and the subscription starts by itself.

Overview

From the shop to a running subscription

Published Plans
Publish the recurring plans you sell online. A product offers the published plans that have a recurring price for it.
Plan Choice
Several plans: the customer picks one on the product page, with its price per period. One plan: applied without asking.
Recurring Prices
Prices per plan, variant and pricelist, in the currency of the shop and with taxes shown as on the shop.
Subscription Cart
The cart becomes a subscription. One plan per cart, one-time products allowed beside it, "then X every month" in the summary.
Saved Payment Method
Only payment methods that can be saved are offered by default; the saved one pays the next invoices.
Starts When Paid
Paying confirms the order and starts the subscription, and the first invoice is created, paid and emailed right away.
Features

A closer look

Shop

Recurring price on the product tiles

✓  A "Subscription" badge and "From 10.00 / month" under the price.
✓  Option to show the recurring price instead of the sales price.
Product page

Pick a plan

✓  Price per plan for the chosen variant, updated when the variant or the plan changes.
✓  Unpublished plans never show on the website.
Cart

One plan per cart

✓  A subscription product with another plan is refused with a clear message.
✓  The cart is never changed silently.
Cart

Recurring lines and amount

✓  "per month" on the recurring lines, one-time products beside them.
✓  Removing the last subscription product turns the cart back into a normal order.
Checkout

Payment method saved

✓  Payment providers that cannot save the payment method are hidden, unless the website allows them.
✓  Works for guests: the payment method is saved for the new customer.
Portal

In the customer portal

✓  The web order is listed under My Subscriptions with its invoices and payment method.
✓  The first invoice is created, paid with the online payment and emailed right after the payment (plans invoiced in advance).
Configuration

Publish a plan

✓  "Published on Website" toggle and ribbon on the recurring plan.
✓  Column in the recurring prices of the product.
Configuration

Website settings

✓  Subscriptions without saved payment method: hide or allow the other providers.
✓  Invoice subscriptions at payment (on by default); off, the daily subscription job creates the first invoice.
✓  Show recurring prices on the shop, per website.
