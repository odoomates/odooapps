Overview

Never miss an expiry date

Document Types
Passport, visa, trade license: a number, an issue date, an expiry date, per kind of record or for any document.
Reminders
To-dos 30, 7 and 0 days before, each sent once; optionally an email to the holder.
Renewals
Renew a document: the new one links to the old one, which is archived.
Extra Files
The front and the back of an identity card, next to the file of the document.
Documents Widget
A card per document and per missing type on the records holding them.
Multi-company
Types shared or per company; documents in the folder of their company.

Questions

Good to know

Does it work for any app?
Yes. A type applies to any kind of record, e.g. employees, vendors or vehicles, or to no record at all, e.g. the trade license of the company.
Who sees the documents?
They follow the access rights of their folders in Documents. The Expiring Documents folder is reached by the Documents administrators.
Can another module use it?
Yes. A model inheriting om.documents.expiry.mixin gets the documents, their counts and the Documents widget, and says who is reminded and who holds them.
Does the old Document Expiry app keep working?
Yes, both work side by side.
