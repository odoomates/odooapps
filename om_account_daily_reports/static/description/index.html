Day Book, Cash Book and Bank Book for Odoo 20 Community

The three books the accountant asks for at the end of the day: every entry of the period in the order it was posted, and the movements of the cash and of the bank accounts with their running balance.

Day Book Cash Book Bank Book Community Edition
Day book

Everything that was posted, day by day

The journal items of the period grouped under the date they were posted on, each day closing with its own debit, credit and balance — the book you print to see what a day was made of.

  • ✓  Date, journal, partner, reference, entry, label, debit, credit, balance and the amount in the foreign currency.
  • ✓  A total per day, so the book balances in front of you.
  • ✓  Every journal of the company by default, or only the ones you pick.
  • ✓  Narrow it down to a few accounts when you are chasing one thing.
  • ✓  The journals, the period and the target moves are printed in the header, so a filed copy says how it was made.

The Day Book: the entries of each day, with the total of the day.

Cash book

The cash, with the balance after every movement

The movements of the cash accounts, grouped by account, each line carrying the balance as it stood after it — what you check the drawer against at the end of the day.

  • ✓  The accounts of the cash journals are filled in for you: the default account of each journal and the accounts of its payment methods.
  • ✓  Each account opens with its own debit, credit and balance for the period.
  • ✓  Add an Initial Balance line to carry in what the account held before the period.
  • ✓  Sort the movements by date, or by journal and partner.
  • ✓  Show every account, only those that moved, or only those whose balance is not zero.

The Cash Book: the movements of the cash account and its running balance.

Bank book

The same, for the bank accounts

The movements of the bank accounts with their running balance, ready to be held against the statement your bank sends.

  • ✓  The accounts of the bank journals and of their payment methods are filled in for you.
  • ✓  The same initial balance, sorting and account options as the cash book.
  • ✓  One book per account, however many bank accounts the company holds.

The Bank Book: the movements of the bank account and its running balance.

Everywhere

The same three questions, on every book

  • ✓  Which period: any start and end date, today by default.
  • ✓  Which entries: the posted ones, or the drafts as well.
  • ✓  Which journals and which accounts.
  • ✓  Printed as a PDF, under Reporting › Daily Reports.
  • ✓  Translated into Arabic, French, Spanish, Turkish, Ukrainian and Chinese.
