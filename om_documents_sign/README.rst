=================
Documents - Sign
=================

Bridge between Documents and Sign.

* Request a signature on a PDF document, from its form, from the selected
  documents or with a workflow action of the folder: a draft signature request
  with the PDF, the contact of the document suggested as the signer.
* Once everyone signed, the signed PDF becomes the new version of the document;
  the PDF sent stays in its versions.
* A Signatures smart button on the documents.
