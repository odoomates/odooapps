Overview

Sign without leaving Documents

Request Signature
A draft signature request with the PDF, the contact as the signer.
Signed Version
The signed PDF becomes the new version; the PDF sent stays in the history.
Actions
From the form, the selected documents or a workflow action of the folder.
Features

A closer look

Document

Signed and versioned

✓  Request Signature on any PDF document.
✓  The Signatures button shows the requests of the document.
✓  Once signed, the signed PDF is the file of the document; the previous one is in the versions.
Request

The request of Sign

✓  Signers, order, deadline and reminders of Sign.
✓  The signers sign from the link emailed to them.
Suite

Documents and its bridges

Documents FREE
Folders with access rights, preview, versions, share and upload links, requests, actions.
Documents - Accounting FREE
Bills, invoices and credit notes from documents; invoice files in a folder.
Documents - Sign FREE
Signature requests from PDF documents; the signed PDF as the new version.
Documents - Project FREE
A folder per project with the files of its tasks, shared with the customer.
Documents - Product FREE
A folder per product with its sheets, manuals and pictures.
Documents - Expiry FREE
Document types, expiry dates, reminders, renewals and a Documents widget on records.
