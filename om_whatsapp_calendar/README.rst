==========================
WhatsApp Meeting Reminders
==========================

WhatsApp reminders of meetings, through the Odoo Mates WhatsApp connector.
Installed by itself when Calendar and the WhatsApp connector are both
installed.

* A new reminder type, **WhatsApp**, with its template (*Calendar: Meeting
  Reminder*) and an optional account; two reminders are ready: one hour and
  one day before.
* Each attendee who has not declined gets the reminder on their mobile number,
  with the time of the meeting in their own time zone and its place or video
  call link. The organizer gets it when the reminder notifies the responsible.
* Empty account: an account of the organizer of the meeting.

Every message is kept on the meeting; opted-out numbers are skipped; a
reminder that cannot go never stops the others.
