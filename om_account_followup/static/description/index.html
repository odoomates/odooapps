Plans

A ladder of levels, triggered by how late the customer is

Each level fires a number of days after the due date and says what should happen: an email, a printed letter, an activity for a colleague, or simply a mark on the customer.

  • ✓  As many levels as your credit policy needs.
  • ✓  The message of each level, with the customer statement attached if you want.
  • ✓  The plan applies to every customer, or only to those you put on it.

A follow-up plan and its levels.

The screen

One line per customer, with everything you need to act

Open a customer and you see every open invoice, how late each one is and the total owed, with the level Odoo proposes and the actions to take.

  • ✓  Record a promise to pay with a date and the customer drops off the list until then.
  • ✓  Mark an invoice disputed so it stops driving reminders while it is argued about.
  • ✓  Exclude a customer you never want chased automatically.
  • ✓  Send every reminder of the day in one action.

The follow-up screen: who is late, and what to do next.

Statements

What the customer needs to see

Print or email a statement of account showing every open invoice, what has been paid and the ageing of the balance.

  • ✓  Sent from the customer, or with the reminder of a level.
  • ✓  A follow-up analysis showing how the overdue balance is spread across the levels.
