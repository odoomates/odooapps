Bridge of eCommerce and WhatsApp Quotations and Orders, installed by itself when both are.

What it does

Through the WhatsApp connector of Odoo Mates, with the provider of your choice.

Order Confirmed
When the payment confirms the order.
Cart Reminder
One WhatsApp to a customer who left a cart.
Back to the Cart
The link opens the cart again.
After the Delay
The delay of the abandoned carts.
Smart
Not after a failed payment or a later order.
Once
Each cart is reminded one time only.
Opt-out
Opted-out numbers get nothing.
Templates
Edit the texts, or pick your own.

Settings
Website > Configuration > Settings: WhatsApp next to the emails.
✓  Order Confirmation: the WhatsApp of the online orders.
✓  Follow up abandoned carts: the WhatsApp reminder.
✓  Only the carts left after it is turned on are reminded.
The reminder on the cart
The products of the cart and the link to resume it, kept in its chatter.
✓  Sent once, after the delay of the abandoned carts.
✓  Checked every hour by a scheduled action.

Works with the WhatsApp Connector

Needs the base; add one provider. Messages go by themselves with Meta and QR; WhatsApp Web sends them from the browser of the user, Click to Chat opens WhatsApp with the text filled in. With Meta, submit the templates for approval first.

WhatsApp Connector Base
Templates, composer and message log: needed by this app.
WhatsApp Connector Meta
The official Cloud API: sends by itself, approved templates.
WhatsApp Connector Web
Texts and PDFs from WhatsApp Web in your browser.
WhatsApp Connector QR
Your WhatsApp number linked by a QR code, sending by itself.
