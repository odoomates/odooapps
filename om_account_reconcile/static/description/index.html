Reconcile

One screen for the bank and the cash

Pick a journal, filter on To Reconcile, Reconciled or All, and search a transaction by label, partner or amount. For the selected transaction you get the open journal items that are likely to be its counterpart, ranked by amount, reference, partner and direction.

  • ✓  Match one item, several items, or only a part of the amount of an item.
  • ✓  Search the open items yourself when no suggestion fits.
  • ✓  Add a counterpart by hand with its account, label, partner, taxes and analytic distribution.
  • ✓  Apply a reconciliation model to fill in the counterparts from its lines.
  • ✓  Preview the journal items and the amount left to cover before validating; what is not covered stays on the suspense account.
  • ✓  Undo a reconciliation, and mark a transaction to check or reviewed for a colleague.

Match items

Match the open items outside the bank

A list of the open items of the reconcilable accounts, grouped by account and partner, with a Match button.

  • ✓  A wizard showing the debit, the credit and the difference of the selected items.
  • ✓  Write the difference off on a journal, account, date and label of your choice.
  • ✓  Without a write-off the items are matched partially.
  • ✓  The Match Suggestions action reconciles, in one go, the groups of items of a same account and partner whose amounts cancel each other.

Match Journal Items, grouped by account and partner.

Automation

Let the new transactions reconcile themselves

  • ✓  Partner mapping models fill in the partner of a new transaction.
  • ✓  Reconciliation models with the automatic trigger are applied on their own.
  • ✓  A transaction matching exactly one open item by amount and reference is reconciled by itself.
  • ✓  A scheduled action treats the transactions in batches every day, and runs again as soon as new transactions are created or imported.
  • ✓  A transaction that fails is logged and does not stop the others.
Dashboard

What is left to do, on the accounting dashboard

The bank and cash cards show a Reconcile button with the number of transactions to reconcile, the balance of the last statement and the transactions marked to check. Type transactions in from the New Transaction button, and the cash counted at the end of the day from the New Statement button of a cash card.

  • ✓  Only users of the accounting group can reconcile.
  • ✓  A transaction or a group of items being reconciled is locked, so two users cannot reconcile it twice.
