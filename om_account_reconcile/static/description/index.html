Features

A closer look

Reconcile

One screen for the bank and the cash

Pick a journal, filter on To Reconcile, Reconciled or All, and search a transaction by label, partner or amount.

✓  Suggested counterparts ranked by amount, reference, partner and direction.
✓  Match one item, several items, or only part of the amount of an item.
✓  Add a counterpart by hand, or apply a reconciliation model to fill it in.
✓  Preview the entry before validating; what is not covered stays on the suspense account.
✓  Undo a reconciliation, and mark a transaction to check or reviewed.
✓  The partner of a new transaction found when it is certain: bank account, exact company name, invoice reference.
✓  Early payment discounts, cheque numbers and payment tolerances in the matching.
✓  Notes, activities and documents of the transaction; a draft bill or invoice from a document.
✓  Several transactions at once: set their partner, or book them on an account.
✓  Rules learned from the transactions booked by hand, and a Bank Fees model per bank.
Match items

Match the open items outside the bank

✓  Open items of the reconcilable accounts, grouped by account and partner.
✓  A wizard with the debit, the credit and the difference of the selection.
✓  Write the difference off on the journal, account, date and label you choose.
✓  Match Suggestions reconciles, in one go, the groups whose amounts cancel out.
✓  Items of two accounts or partners moved together first, taxes taken out of a write-off.
✓  Reconcile Automatically: the perfect pairs, or a partner's items adding up to zero.
✓  A bank transaction matched straight from the outstanding credits of the invoice.
Dashboard

What is left to do, on the accounting dashboard

✓  A Reconcile button on the bank and cash cards, with the count to reconcile.
✓  The last statement and the transactions to review, one click away.
✓  New Transaction on every card, and New Statement for the cash count of the day.
✓  A transaction being reconciled is locked, so two users cannot reconcile it twice.
How it works

In three steps

1
Bring in the transactions
Type them in from the dashboard, or import the statement of your bank.
2
Reconcile
Take a suggestion, apply a model, or let automatic reconciliation do it.
3
Prove the balance
Print the Bank Reconciliation Statement at the close.

Install

Get started

How to install it

1Copy the module to a folder of your own modules, e.g. /opt/odoo/custom-addons, and add that folder to addons_path in odoo.conf.
2Restart Odoo.
3Activate the developer mode, then Apps → Update Apps List.
4Clear the Apps filter, search om_account_reconcile and install it.
Do not use Apps → Import Module. It is meant for data modules without Python, and leaves this module half loaded.

Questions we get

Can new transactions reconcile themselves?
Yes. Turn on automatic reconciliation in the settings: a daily scheduled action applies the partner mappings, the reconciliation models with the automatic trigger, and the exact matches by amount and reference. A transaction that fails is logged and does not stop the others.
How do I import the statement of my bank?
Install om_account_bank_statement_import for CAMT.053, MT940, OFX, QIF, CSV and Excel files; the imported transactions are reconciled on this screen.
Do I need the full accounting app?
No. Bank Reconciliation works on its own. For complete accounting, install om_account_accountant, which brings it together with the rest of the suite.
Do I need Odoo Enterprise?
No. Everything on this page runs on the Community Edition.
